# Figure sources and licences

All figures in this deck are published under Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) licences. They are redistributed here cropped and resized (longer side at most 650 px, PNG) with attribution to the articles below. They are not covered by the repository's MIT licence; each remains under its article's CC BY licence and the copyright of its authors (Rangus et al.: World Stroke Organization).

Licence text was read from the PMC full-text XML of each article (NCBI E-utilities, `<license>` element and the licence sentence), checked against the Crossref licence field of the DOI. Publisher pages were read only for Kizilgoz (Nature), Chen and Wang (Frontiers); the Springer, BMC and SAGE pages could not be read (bot check, HTTP 403) and were not bypassed. Captions were read; the figure images themselves were not inspected for in-image credits. Check date: 2026-11-23.

Crop boxes are fractions of the source figure (left, top, right, bottom). "Whole figure" means no crop. Nothing was redrawn or relabelled.

## Notes

- Kizilgoz Figs 3 and 4: the article states that the classification was modified from Chen et al., Chin J Radiol 2004. The slide credits say so ("classification after Chen et al. 2004"). Under the licence stated for the article these figures are covered; the 2004 source was not checked.
- Rangus: the copyright holder is the World Stroke Organization; the licence is CC BY 4.0, not a non-commercial one.
- Greggio: only Fig 2 is used. Fig 1 of that article carries a BioRender credit and is not used.
- What each figure shows follows the article's own caption.
