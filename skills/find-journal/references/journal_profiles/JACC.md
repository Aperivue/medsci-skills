## Scope
The Journal of the American College of Cardiology (JACC) is the flagship journal of the American College of Cardiology. It publishes original peer-reviewed clinical and experimental reports on all aspects of cardiovascular disease, including coronary artery and valve disease, congenital heart defects, vascular surgery, cardiomyopathy, drug treatment, new diagnostic techniques, findings from the laboratory, and large multi-center studies of new therapies, for a readership of cardiologists and cardiovascular researchers worldwide.

## Special Notes
The ACC flagship journal, published weekly. Single-blind peer review; Original Investigations are capped at **5,000 words** (including text, references, and figure legends) with a **structured 250-word abstract (Background / Objectives / Methods / Results / Conclusions)** plus a condensed abstract of no more than 100 words stressing clinical implications. A Central Illustration and a Clinical Perspectives section (clinical competencies plus translational outlook) are required for Original Investigations. Case reports are generally not considered. Expedited review is available for Original Investigations of high-potential clinical impact (suitability decision within 2 days, initial decision within 14 days). AI policy: the journal's guide displays no journal-specific AI policy; Elsevier publisher-level policy applies — AI tools cannot be listed as authors, AI use in writing must be disclosed in a declaration immediately before the references, and AI must not be used to create or alter primary research images or graphical abstracts.
