## Scope
Circulation is the AHA's flagship clinical cardiology journal, publishing basic, translational, clinical, and population-health research aimed at improving the health of people at risk for, or living with, cardiovascular disease. All submissions must clearly articulate their relevance to cardiovascular disease and carry a message that appeals to a broad clinical and scientific readership. Mechanistic or basic-science work without a direct clinical link belongs in Circulation Research. Circulation publishes the AHA/ACC clinical practice guidelines that define cardiovascular care worldwide.

## Special Notes
The AHA flagship journal and one of the most-cited cardiovascular journals worldwide. 2025 Journal Impact Factor 41.3 (Clarivate 2026 JCR release); manuscript acceptance rate 8% (AHA); 17 days from submission to first decision for Original Research Articles (AHA). Original Research requires a Clinical Perspective ("What Is New?" / "What Are the Clinical Implications?", 2–3 bullets each, ≤100 words each) and a Data Availability Statement. Revision window 90 days for Original Research and Research Letters. Acceptance to Publish Ahead of Print 7–10 days; acceptance to print 81 days. AI policy (AHA-wide): generative AI is permitted with documentation, cannot be an author (ICMJE), must be listed in Acknowledgments, documented in Methods if used in research design, analysis, or figure preparation, exempt for grammar/spell-check only; AI image generation/manipulation is prohibited. Choose Circulation over Circulation Research when the work is clinical or translational with direct practice implications; choose Circulation Research for mechanistic/basic science. Rejected work may be offered transfer within the AHA journal family.
