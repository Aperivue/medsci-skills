# ESMO Open

## Identity
- **Abbreviation:** ESMO Open
- **Publisher:** Elsevier, on behalf of the European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO)
- **ISSN:** 2059-7029 (online only)
- **Homepage:** https://www.esmoopen.com/
- **Author guidelines:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/esmo-open/publish/guide-for-authors

## Scope
ESMO Open is an online-only, open access oncology journal dedicated to high-quality medical research and educational content from all disciplines of oncology, with a focus on innovative clinical and translational cancer research. It covers experimental, translational, diagnostic, and therapeutic areas and operates a fast submission and continuous online publication process.

## Acceptance Signals
- **Selectivity band:** selective
- **Desk-reject triggers:** limited novelty or clinical relevance; poor fit with the innovative clinical/translational focus; missing mandatory ethics, funding, conflict-of-interest, or trial-registration statements
- **Design expectations:** CONSORT checklist and flow diagram required for clinical trials; real-world data studies should follow ESMO-GROW with its checklist; sex and gender dimensions addressed per SAGER
- **Study-type tolerance:** values clinical trial results, real-world evidence with an ESMO-relevant message, biomarker and treatment-outcome analyses, supportive-care and practice-relevant studies
- **Review process:** single blind, typically two external reviewers; continuous online publication
- **Cascade / transfer:** common transfer destination from Annals of Oncology (reviewer comments carried over, independent assessment, APC applies)

## Special Notes
Original Articles are up to 4,000 words with a structured abstract up to 300 words, up to 100 references, and up to 5 figures and tables; the trial registration number goes at the end of the abstract; optional Highlights are 3 to 5 items of at most 125 characters each. Cover letters should be under one page and state what is new and why it fits the journal. The journal homepage reports an impact factor of 10.6 (JCR 2025) and an APC of USD 4,320 excluding taxes; confirm current values on the journal site.

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy
- **Requirement level:** Required when generative AI or AI-assisted technologies are used in the writing process
- **Permitted scope:** Only to improve readability and language; not for summarizing or interpreting data, drawing scientific insights or conclusions, or making recommendations; human oversight mandatory; AI cannot be an author
- **Disclosure location:** Dedicated section before the references titled "Declaration of Generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process"; no declaration needed for basic grammar, spelling, or reference tools
- **AI-generated images:** Not addressed in the guide for authors
- **Policy URL:** https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals
