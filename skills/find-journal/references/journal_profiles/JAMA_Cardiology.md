## Scope
JAMA Cardiology is an international peer-reviewed journal and the definitive journal for clinical investigators, clinicians, and trainees in cardiovascular medicine worldwide. It publishes exceptional original research, state-of-the-art reviews, and informative opinions that advance the science and practice of cardiology, enhance cardiovascular health, and inform health care policy, across all aspects of cardiovascular medicine including epidemiology and prevention, diagnostic testing, interventional and pharmacologic therapeutics, translational research, health care policy and outcomes, and global health.

## Special Notes
A JAMA Network specialty journal, published online weekly with 12 print/online issues a year. Highly selective (8% acceptance; median 5 days to first decision without external review, 37 days with review). Health equity, when supported by original science, is a leading editorial priority. Papers not accepted may be referred to another JAMA Network specialty journal or JAMA Network Open with reviewers' comments. AI policy: AI assistance with writing must be disclosed in the Acknowledgment (Methods if AI is part of the research design); AI must not draft Opinion pieces, Letters, or Online Comments and must not generate references; AI-created clinical images are not permitted unless part of formal research design and fully disclosed.
