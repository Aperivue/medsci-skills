## Scope
AJNR publishes articles on the clinical imaging, therapy, and basic science of the central and peripheral nervous system, including the brain, spine, head and neck, interventional procedures, techniques in neuroimaging and neurointervention, and related educational, socioeconomic, and medicolegal issues. It gives particular attention to prospective investigations that test specific hypotheses.

## Special Notes
AJNR's scope covers both diagnostic and interventional neuroradiology; Original Research allows a 350-word structured abstract and 4000 words, and after acceptance an Original Research article needs a three-cell Summary Section table (Previous literature / Key findings / Knowledge advancement). Case reports are not an AJNR article type (they go to the AJNR Case Collection), and Clinical Reports are series of more than five cases. AI policy: LLMs cannot be authors; LLM use must be documented in Methods or as an acknowledgment, naming the tool, version, and developer and the content created or edited. For AI studies AJNR recommends reporting the study's level of evidence and depositing code in a public repository by acceptance.
