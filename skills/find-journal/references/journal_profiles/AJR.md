## Identity
- **Abbreviation:** AJR Am J Roentgenol

## Scope
AJR publishes original research, systematic reviews, reviews, and research letters across all subspecialties of diagnostic radiology, with strong emphasis on clinical imaging practice, diagnostic accuracy studies, and AI applications in medical imaging.

## Scope Keywords
diagnostic radiology, body imaging, CT, MRI, ultrasound, abdominal imaging, thoracic imaging, musculoskeletal imaging, breast imaging, genitourinary imaging, pediatric radiology, AI in radiology, diagnostic accuracy, imaging biomarkers, contrast media, interventional radiology

## Special Notes
AJR is one of the oldest and most widely read general radiology journals, published by ARRS since 1906. It requires Highlights for original research: a Key Finding (maximum 50 words) and its Importance (maximum 25 words). Strong emphasis on clinical relevance and practical applicability of findings. AI policy: follows ICMJE — disclose AI use in Methods.

## Acceptance Signals
- **Selectivity band:** selective
- **Desk-reject triggers:** limited clinical relevance or practical applicability; incremental work that cannot fill a concrete Key Finding statement
- **Design expectations:** clinically grounded diagnostic-accuracy or imaging studies with a clearly articulated contribution
- **Study-type tolerance:** original research and research letters valued when clinically actionable
- **Review process:** double-blind; mandatory Highlights (Key Finding + Importance); ICMJE AI disclosure
- **Cascade / transfer:** tier-down to a specialty or subspecialty radiology journal
