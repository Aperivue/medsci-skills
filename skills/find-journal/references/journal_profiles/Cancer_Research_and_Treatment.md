## Scope
Cancer Research and Treatment is a peer-reviewed open access journal covering experimental and clinical cancer research. Its stated scope includes carcinogenesis, tumor biology, molecular oncology, genetics, immunology, epidemiology, biomarkers, prevention, pathology, diagnosis, screening, chemotherapy, surgery, radiation therapy, immunotherapy, gene therapy, multimodality treatment, and palliative care.

## Acceptance Signals
- **Selectivity band:** accessible
- **Desk-reject triggers:** limited originality or importance to experimental or clinical cancer research; unregistered interventional clinical trial; incomplete ethics, IRB, consent, or data-sharing information; missing mandatory AI-use declaration; inadequate English without professional language review
- **Design expectations:** reporting-guideline adherence recommended (CONSORT, STARD, PRISMA, STROBE, MOOSE); Ethical Statement with IRB title and approval number; author contributions in the journal's fixed categories
- **Study-type tolerance:** values solid clinical or translational oncology across tumor types, including Korean or Asian multicenter evidence with international relevance; case reports discouraged unless uniquely significant
- **Review process:** double-blind; initial decision normally within 4 weeks; revision due within 12 weeks
- **Cascade / transfer:** no formal transfer program; a practical one-tier-down option after a desk reject at a highly selective international oncology venue

## Special Notes
Original Articles have no explicit body word limit but require a 250-word abstract (Purpose, Materials and Methods, Results, Conclusion), fewer than 30 references, and at most 10 tables plus figures combined. Manuscripts from non-English-speaking countries must be revised by a professional linguistic reviewer, stated in the cover letter. The author guidelines list an article processing charge of USD 600 per article (USD 300 for case reports, correspondence, or errata); confirm current values on the journal site.

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy
- **Requirement level:** Required
- **Permitted scope:** Generative AI may be used to enhance readability and language accuracy; chatbots and AI tools cannot be authors; AI-generated material cannot be cited as a primary source
- **Disclosure location:** Declared at submission and clearly reported in the manuscript, specifying tool, model name, version, manufacturer, and the capacity in which it was used
- **AI-generated images:** Not permitted for creating or altering images, except where AI is integral to the research methods and described in Methods; basic brightness, contrast, or color adjustments acceptable
- **Policy URL:** https://www.e-crt.org/authors/authors.php
