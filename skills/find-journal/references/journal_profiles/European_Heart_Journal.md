## Scope
The European Heart Journal is the official international peer-reviewed journal of the European Society of Cardiology. It publishes the highest quality clinical and scientific material on all aspects of cardiovascular medicine, including original research findings, state-of-the-art reviews, and the ESC Clinical Practice Guidelines, for a readership spanning the whole cardiology community.

## Special Notes
The ESC flagship journal, published 48 times a year. Single-anonymised peer review with specialist statistical reviewers; Fast Track review (initial decision within 7 working days) for selected clinical, translational and meta-analysis papers, including congress-timed work. Research articles require a mandatory structured graphical abstract (Key Question, Key Finding, Take-home Message) alongside a 250-word structured text abstract; initial submissions may be format-free. Papers not accepted may be transferred to another ESC journal with reviewer reports carried over. AI policy: AI tools do not qualify as authors; any AI use (content, images, code, data processing, translation) must be disclosed in the cover letter at submission and explained in full in the Methods or Acknowledgements section.
