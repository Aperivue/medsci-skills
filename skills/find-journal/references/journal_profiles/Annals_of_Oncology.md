## Scope
Annals of Oncology publishes findings of particular significance in clinical oncology and clinically oriented cancer research. Acceptance rests on originality and high scientific quality, with strong emphasis on clinical relevance; mechanistic or biomarker work without clear clinical translation is generally not a fit.

## Acceptance Signals
- **Selectivity band:** highly-selective
- **Desk-reject triggers:** insufficient priority or significance; weak clinical relevance; mechanistic work without clinical translation; biomarker studies without actionable clinical utility
- **Design expectations:** reporting-guideline compliance is mandatory where applicable (CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA, REMARK, TRIPOD, ESMO-GROW for real-world data) with checklists submitted as separate files; phase III trials may undergo statistical review
- **Study-type tolerance:** values practice-relevant trials, pivotal biomarker analyses, and translational findings with direct clinical actionability; deprioritizes descriptive single-center series
- **Review process:** editorial office aims to return low-priority papers within 3 weeks and notify authors within 6 weeks; presubmission enquiry strongly encouraged through the Editorial Manager database
- **Cascade / transfer:** transfer to ESMO Open carries the Annals reviewer comments but is assessed independently and carries an APC

## Special Notes
Original Articles are 4,500 words with a 300-word structured abstract (Background, Patients and Methods, Results, Conclusions) and a mandatory Key Message of at most 400 characters; each table or figure counts as 150 words toward the limit and no more than six in total are recommended. Manuscripts exceeding limits by no more than 10% are not automatically rejected. The journal homepage reports an impact factor of 80.4 (JCR 2025 release); confirm current values on the journal site.
