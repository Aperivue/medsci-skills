## Scope
Journal of Breast Cancer is the official journal of the Korean Breast Cancer Society. It publishes clinical, translational, epidemiologic, and basic research relevant to breast cancer, and may consider clinically useful work on benign breast disease. Regional data are welcome when presented with international relevance.

## Acceptance Signals
- **Selectivity band:** accessible
- **Desk-reject triggers:** out-of-scope manuscript without a breast-cancer, benign-breast-disease, or clinically useful breast-health focus; duplicate publication or plagiarism concerns (CrossCheck screening); unmasked author or institutional identifiers under double-blind review
- **Design expectations:** IRB approval, informed consent, and clinical-trial registration statements where applicable; EQUATOR reporting guidance recommended, SPIRIT for trial protocols
- **Study-type tolerance:** values breast-specific clinical, epidemiologic, translational, and surgical studies, including well-conducted single-center or multicenter cohorts and Korean or Asian data with international relevance
- **Review process:** double-blind; initial editorial assessment followed by two to three external reviewers; title page submitted separately
- **Cascade / transfer:** no formal transfer program; a natural fallback for breast-specific manuscripts declined at international breast or oncology journals

## Special Notes
Original Articles are 4,000 words with a 300-word structured abstract (Purpose, Methods, Results, Conclusion); the instructions state no reference cap for Original Articles. Brief Communications are 2,000 words with at most two tables and two figures. Author and institutional identifiers must be masked outside the separately submitted title page. The journal homepage reports an impact factor of 2.4 (2024); confirm current values on the journal site.
