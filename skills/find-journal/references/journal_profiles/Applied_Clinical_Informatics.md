## Scope
ACI is the official eJournal of AMIA, IMIA, and EFMI for applied clinical informatics: it publishes work that uses informatics knowledge, concepts, instruments, and methods to improve patient care through the enhanced use of information systems, with an emphasis on translational, practical deployment, evaluation, and lessons learned from systems in use.

## Special Notes
ACI positions itself as the "practical" informatics journal in Thieme's IMIA family, against Methods of Information in Medicine as the "Science and Research" journal: choose ACI when the contribution is a deployed system, its evaluation, or lessons learned from practice, rather than a methods advance. It is double-anonymized, requires a Clinical Relevance Statement and 2-4 multiple-choice questions with every research paper, and publishes about 100 peer-reviewed articles a year. AI policy: a Declaration of Generative AI Use is mandatory at submission (Thieme publisher policy, COPE-aligned); AI-generated images are generally not permitted.
