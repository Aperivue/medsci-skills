## Scope
npj Breast Cancer considers research across all aspects of biology and medicine relevant to breast cancer, including molecular classification, clinical trials and clinical research, immunotherapy and other treatments, carcinogenesis and hereditary susceptibility, molecular epidemiology, screening and prevention, survivorship, metastasis mechanisms, tumor microenvironment, breast imaging, and mechanism-based targeted therapy.

## Acceptance Signals
- **Selectivity band:** selective
- **Desk-reject triggers:** insufficient novelty or significance for a specialized advance; weak technical quality; missing mandatory data availability, ethics, consent, or trial-registration information
- **Design expectations:** a Statistics and Reproducibility section with test names, n per analysis, justification, alpha, tailedness, and exact p-values; Nature Portfolio Reporting Summary with the revised manuscript; CONSORT for randomized trials and ARRIVE for preclinical animal work in Supplementary Information
- **Study-type tolerance:** values specialized advances with new insight (molecular, translational, clinical research with a mechanistic or precision-medicine angle) and well-designed real-world or clinical cohorts with a clear novelty claim; systematic reviews and meta-analyses must be submitted as Articles
- **Review process:** single-anonymized; presubmission enquiries accepted with at least an abstract
- **Cascade / transfer:** automated transfer within the Nature Portfolio offered on decline, with referee reports transferable on request

## Special Notes
Articles use an unstructured abstract of 150 words and a title of 15 words or fewer; the Discussion may not contain subheadings, a limitations section, or a conclusions section, and funding is declared in Acknowledgments rather than a separate funding statement. Nature-style figure rules apply (lowercase bold panel labels, sans-serif type, no red-green contrast). The journal metrics page reports an impact factor of 8.4 (2025) and an APC of GBP 2,990 / USD 3,890 / EUR 3,290 for original research; confirm current values on the journal site.

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy
- **Requirement level:** Tiered (Nature Portfolio policy): assistive use permitted with recommended disclosure; evaluative or interpretive use permitted only with disclosure and demonstrable human oversight; use that replaces human judgment not permitted
- **Permitted scope:** Language polishing, structuring, translation, and data cleaning are assistive; suggesting analytic approaches or drafting explanatory summaries require verification and accountability; generating hypotheses, analyses, or conclusions presented as human-derived, fabricating data or citations, and delegating peer review are prohibited; AI cannot be an author
- **Disclosure location:** Methods section, or a suitable alternative if there is no Methods section
- **AI-generated images:** Photorealistic AI images and deepfakes are not permitted
- **Policy URL:** https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/editorial-policies/ai
