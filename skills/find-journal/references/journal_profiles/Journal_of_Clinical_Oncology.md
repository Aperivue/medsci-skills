## Scope
Journal of Clinical Oncology is ASCO's flagship journal and publishes clinical oncology research across treatment, diagnosis, prevention, survivorship, symptom management, and health services and policy. It prioritizes definitive, practice-informing evidence, especially randomized and large comparative clinical trials.

## Acceptance Signals
- **Selectivity band:** highly-selective
- **Desk-reject triggers:** insufficient practice-changing importance; scope misfit with clinical practice relevance; hypothesis-generating or single-arm early-phase work without definitive implications; weak comparative design
- **Design expectations:** randomized or large, well-powered comparative studies; pre-specified analysis plans; REMARK-compliant biomarker studies; demographic subgroup characterization (age, race, ethnicity, sex) for Original Reports
- **Study-type tolerance:** values phase III and large comparative trials, practice-relevant health-services research; deprioritizes single-center retrospective series and incremental real-world descriptions
- **Review process:** ~30 days to first decision per the author FAQ; mandatory CONSORT/STROBE/PRISMA/REMARK/TRIPOD+AI as applicable; presubmission inquiries accepted for high-profile or time-sensitive work; Rapid Review pathway for late-breaking practice-relevant manuscripts
- **Cascade / transfer:** ASCO sibling journals (JCO Precision Oncology, JCO Oncology Practice, JCO Global Oncology) or a broad oncology venue after rejection; a Fast-Track Presubmission pathway exists for manuscripts previously reviewed at other high-profile journals

## Special Notes
Strict limits: Original Reports are 3,000 words with a 275-word structured abstract (PURPOSE / PATIENTS AND METHODS / RESULTS / CONCLUSION) and at most 6 figures and tables; a graphical abstract is required for Original Reports. Kaplan-Meier plots must show numbers at risk and, for trials, numbers censored. The journal reports an impact factor of 44.7 (JCR 2025); confirm current values on the journal site.

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy
- **Requirement level:** Required for writing assistance and for AI used in data analysis; no disclosure needed for grammar, spelling, formatting, or reference tools
- **Permitted scope:** Writing assistance only for Original Reports and Clinical Trial Updates; not permitted for opinion pieces, Editorials, Oncology Grand Rounds, Understanding the Pathway, or targeted Reviews; AI cannot be an author; reviewers may not use AI
- **Disclosure location:** Methods for data-analysis use; Acknowledgments for writing assistance (tool, version, date accessed, manufacturer, and how it was used)
- **AI-generated images:** Not addressed in the JCO policy
