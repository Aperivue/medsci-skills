## Scope
Clinical Cancer Research publishes innovative clinical and translational cancer research that bridges the laboratory and the clinic. Topics include targeted therapies, mechanisms of drug sensitivity and resistance, pharmacogenomics, personalized medicine, immunotherapy, gene therapy, diagnostic biomarkers, innovative imaging, and clinical genetics, with emphasis on clinical trials that include mechanistic investigation.

## Acceptance Signals
- **Selectivity band:** highly-selective
- **Desk-reject triggers:** scope misfit (purely preclinical mechanism without a clinical bridge, or clinical description without translational insight); missing conflict-of-interest statement, which blocks entry into peer review; weak novelty or significance
- **Design expectations:** mandatory 120-150 word Translational Relevance statement; mandatory data availability statement; statistical reporting of n per data point, biological versus technical replicates, randomization, blinding, and sample-size justification; CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA, or STARD as applicable
- **Study-type tolerance:** values clinical trials with mechanistic or correlative investigation and biomarker studies with defined clinical application; deprioritizes purely clinical outcomes analyses without a translational component and cell-line-only mechanism
- **Review process:** single-blind; presubmission inquiries accepted (brief description, significance, abstract, prepared figures)
- **Cascade / transfer:** declined manuscripts can be transferred to another AACR journal with reviewer comments carried over (Cancer Discovery, Cancer Research, Molecular Cancer Therapeutics, Molecular Cancer Research)

## Special Notes
Research Articles are 5,000 words with a 250-word structured abstract (Purpose, Experimental Design, Results, Conclusions), up to 50 references, and up to 6 combined figures and tables; Methods text does not count toward the word limit and Results and Discussion must not be combined. The AACR metrics page reports an impact factor of 10.9 (JCR); confirm current values on the journal site.

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy
- **Requirement level:** Required when generative AI is used
- **Permitted scope:** AI may revise or translate author-written text; AI that produces new text, images, or material must be described in detail; AI used in the conduct of the research must be described in Methods; AI cannot be a co-author; reviewers should not use generative AI
- **Disclosure location:** Acknowledgments for all use; cover letter additionally for AI-generated new content; Methods for research use
- **AI-generated images:** Permitted only if disclosed, with the tool credited in Acknowledgments
- **Policy URL:** https://aacrjournals.org/pages/editorial-policies#ai
