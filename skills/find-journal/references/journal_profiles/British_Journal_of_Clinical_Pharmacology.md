## Scope
Published on behalf of the British Pharmacological Society, BJCP publishes papers and reports on all aspects of drug action in humans, across all sub-areas of clinical pharmacology, all diseases and all populations, including first-in-human/Phase 1 studies. It welcomes translational papers with a major clinical component but does not publish purely basic pharmacology papers.

## Special Notes
The British Pharmacological Society's clinical journal; choose it over its sister journal British Journal of Pharmacology when the work studies drug action in humans, since BJCP's Senior Editors may offer to transfer out-of-scope work to BJP. It does not normally accept pharmacokinetics-only papers on a new medicine without a measure of pharmacodynamics, requires the GCP-defined Principal Investigator of a human interventional study to be an author, and requires a READUS-PV checklist for disproportionality analyses. Rejected papers may be offered a cascade to Pharmacology Research & Perspectives. AI policy: AIGC tools may be used in writing; describe their use in detail in the Methods or Acknowledgements; spelling and grammar tools are exempt; AI tools cannot be authors.
