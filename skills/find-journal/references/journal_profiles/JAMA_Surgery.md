# JAMA Surgery

## Identity
- **Abbreviation:** JAMA Surg
- **Publisher:** American Medical Association (AMA), JAMA Network
- **ISSN:** 2168-6254 / 2168-6262

## Scope
JAMA Surgery is an international peer-reviewed surgery journal of the JAMA Network, published since 1920, whose mission is "to promote the art and science of surgery" through rigorous, clinically impactful research that improves surgical care. It is the official publication of the Association of VA Surgeons, the Pacific Coast Surgical Association, and the Surgical Outcomes Club.

## Scope Keywords
general surgery, surgical oncology, surgical outcomes, perioperative care, postoperative complications, trauma surgery, transplantation, vascular surgery, minimally invasive surgery, robotic surgery, surgical quality improvement, health services research, surgical education, surgical workforce, randomized clinical trials, comparative effectiveness, cost-effectiveness, surgical innovation, emerging surgical technology

## Article Types Accepted
- Original Investigation

- Letter in Reply

## Classification
- **Tier:** Q1 (the journal describes itself as "the highest ranking surgery journal in the world" by Journal Impact Factor)
- **Open Access:** Hybrid (research articles free 12 months after publication with no author fee; immediate open access for "Payment of $6000", under CC-BY-NC-ND, or CC-BY where a not-for-profit funder requires it)
- **Field:** Surgery (general and all surgical subspecialties)

## Acceptance Signals
- **Selectivity band:** highly selective (the journal states "The journal's acceptance rate is 9%.")
- **Desk-reject triggers:** missing Key Points (not required for Research Letters or Narrative Reviews) or data sharing statement on a research manuscript; no completed reporting checklist where the journal requires one (CONSORT for trials, TREND for nonrandomized trials, PRISMA for systematic reviews and meta-analyses, MOOSE for meta-analyses of observational studies; other designs are asked to follow the EQUATOR guideline); a systematic review without meta-analysis or a narrative review submitted without the required presubmission inquiry; clinical images created or manipulated by AI outside a disclosed research design
- **Design expectations:** EQUATOR guideline per study type (CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA/MOOSE, STARD/TRIPOD, CHEERS, SQUIRE, SRQR/COREQ); power or sample size statement for randomized trials; survey response rates generally expected to be at least 60%
- **Study-type tolerance:** randomized and nonrandomized trials, cohort, case-control, cross-sectional, case series, economic evaluations, decision models, quality improvement, survey and qualitative studies are all listed study types
- **Review process:** single-anonymized; the journal states "The median time to first decision is 11 days, and 41 days with review."
- **Cascade / transfer:** JAMA Network journal; authors of papers not accepted "may have the option" to have the paper and reviewers' comments "automatically referred to one of the JAMA Network specialty journals and/or JAMA Network Open"

## Special Notes
Original Investigations are limited to "3000 words of text" with "no more than a total of 5 tables and/or figures" and a structured abstract "of no more than 350 words" in JAMA Network headings; 3-part Key Points (Question, Findings, Meaning, 75-100 words) are required for research manuscripts, meta-analyses and Systematic Reviews, not for Research Letters or Narrative Reviews. Study-type tables list 50-75 references for most designs; Research Letters are 800 words, at most 10 references and 2 small tables/figures, with no abstract. The journal states "The Journal Impact Factor is 15.6"; the JCR year is not given on that page. AI policy: AI use in manuscript preparation must be reported in the Acknowledgment section (description of the content created or edited, tool name, version and extension numbers, manufacturer, date(s) of use, and confirmation that the authors take responsibility for it); AI may not generate or format references, draft Opinion manuscripts, Letters to the Editor or Online Comments, or create clinical images.

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy
- **Requirement level:** Required
- **Permitted scope:** Authors "must review and confirm the accuracy of all content generated by artificial intelligence (AI)"; "Use of AI, LLM, and chatbots to draft Opinion manuscripts, Letters to the Editor, and Online Comments is not permitted", nor may AI "generate or format references"; AI tools "do not qualify for authorship"
- **Disclosure location:** Acknowledgment section for manuscript preparation ("a description of the content that was created or edited", the tool's name, version and extension numbers, manufacturer, "date(s) of use", and confirmation that the authors take responsibility for the integrity of the content generated); Methods when AI is part of the research
- **AI-generated images:** Not permitted for clinical images and clinical illustrations unless part of a formal, disclosed research design
