## Scope
JAMIA is AMIA's premier peer-reviewed journal for biomedical and health informatics. Covering the full spectrum of activities in the field, it includes informatics articles in the areas of clinical care, clinical research, translational science, implementation science, imaging, education, consumer health, public health, and policy, describing innovative informatics research and systems that help to advance biomedical science and to promote health.

## Special Notes
AMIA's flagship journal, and the natural home for work whose contribution is the informatics method, system, or implementation itself — choose it over a general medical journal when the informatics contribution, not a clinical finding, is the point. It also welcomes implementation, policy, and education pieces that methods-focused informatics journals would turn away. AI policy: AI tools do not qualify as authors; disclose AI use in the cover letter and in the Methods or Acknowledgements section. Creating or altering primary factual images, such as clinical or diagnostic images, is not permitted.
