# Multicenter Prospective Cohort with External Validation

## Methods
A circular region of interest was placed on the right hepatic lobe on each scan.
Confounding was handled with a marginal structural model.
Area deprivation index served as a proxy for socioeconomic status (covariate only).
FIB-4 was included as a baseline covariate; the endpoint was biopsy-proven fibrosis.
Technical feasibility of the acquisition protocol was recorded for every site.
