# Journal Profile: Journal of Clinical Oncology

## Journal Identity

- **Full name**: Journal of Clinical Oncology
- **Abbreviation**: J Clin Oncol (JCO)
- **Publisher**: American Society of Clinical Oncology (ASCO)
- **ISSN**: 0732-183X (print), 1527-7755 (online)
- **Frequency**: Three times per month, 36 issues per year
- **Impact Factor**: 44.7 (JCR 2025, as reported on the ASCO author pages; confirm current value)
- **Open Access**: Hybrid. Gold OA with APC (CC BY or CC BY-NC-ND); Original Reports become free to read 12 months after online publication
- **Acceptance rate**: ~11% for Original Reports (ASCO author FAQ)
- **Peer review**: Not stated on the author pages; ~30 days to first decision per the FAQ

## Abstract Requirements

Original Reports require a structured abstract of 275 words:

```text
PURPOSE
PATIENTS AND METHODS
RESULTS
CONCLUSION
```

- Include the most important data with specific numbers rather than vague claims.
- Do not use proprietary or trade names in the title or abstract.
- For clinical trials, the ClinicalTrials.gov identifier must appear in the abstract itself.
- Do not overstate results or understate toxicities.

---

## Required Sections (Original Report)

1. **Title page**
   - Title of at most 175 characters including spaces, naming disease, drug, and trial where applicable, without alluding to results
   - Authors with degrees and affiliations; corresponding author contact
   - Running head of at most 65 characters
   - Prior presentations or publications, disclaimers, funding
2. **Abstract and keywords**
3. **Introduction**
4. **Patients and Methods** (ICMJE-compliant structure)
5. **Results**
6. **Discussion**
7. **Mandatory statements**
   - Data sharing statement for clinical trial manuscripts reporting clinical outcome data
   - Clinical trial registration ID and URL, registered at or before first patient enrollment
   - Ethics/IRB approval and informed consent, consistent with the Declaration of Helsinki
   - Author contributions per ICMJE criteria
   - Conflict-of-interest disclosures through the ASCO COI database
8. **References, tables, figures**

For interventional studies, submit the full protocol or, at minimum, eligibility criteria, schema and treatment plan, dose modification rules, response criteria, stopping rules, and the statistical section as a supplemental file.

## Statistical Reporting

- Report actual p-values, never "not significant". Two decimals suffice for p > .01; report three decimals for p < .01; p < .001 is acceptable for very small values.
- Use two-sided p-values unless the design explicitly specifies one-sided testing; if one-sided or Bayesian, also give the two-sided p-value of the standard test.
- Report 95% confidence intervals for primary outcomes, odds ratios, and hazard ratios, in the style "HR, 0.73; 95% CI, 0.60 to 0.89; P = .002".
- Report point estimates with both absolute and relative differences where appropriate.
- Disclose the statistical software and version.
- Address multiple comparisons and label exploratory analyses; the statistical analysis plan should be pre-specified.
- Kaplan-Meier plots must include numbers at risk, and for clinical trial outcomes also numbers censored per arm.
- Required reporting guidelines: CONSORT (randomized trials; diagram plus checklist, diagram does not count toward the figure limit), STROBE, MOOSE, PRISMA, CHEERS, REMARK for biomarker studies, TRIPOD+AI for prediction models, and the CONSORT PRO extension for patient-reported outcomes.
- Original Reports must characterize demographic subgroups at minimum by age, race, ethnicity, and sex, and transparently note representativeness limitations.

---

## Figures

- **Maximum**: 6 figures and tables combined for Original Reports, Reviews, and Special Articles; the CONSORT diagram does not count.
- **Resolution**: Photographs and halftones at minimum 300 dpi at final size after acceptance.
- **Format**: At submission, EPS, GIF, PNG, TIFF, JPEG, PDF, or PowerPoint. Not accepted: BMP, PICT, XLS, PSD, AI, CDR, LaTeX, OLE-embedded, or locked PDFs. Vector artwork preferred after acceptance.
- **Type**: 6-12 pt Univers, with Arial or Helvetica acceptable.
- **Graphical abstract**: Required for JCO Original Reports. One figure with a legend and a context/relevance summary under 100 words, at minimum 300 dpi.
- **Color**: Not stated on the format pages.

## Common Rejection Reasons

_Editorial-risk summary drawn from the official guidance and stated editorial priorities._

1. Insufficient practice-changing importance; hypothesis-generating or single-arm early-phase work rarely clears the bar.
2. Scope misfit with clinical oncology practice relevance.
3. Methodologic weakness: underpowered or non-comparative design, unaddressed multiple comparisons, or post hoc analyses presented as confirmatory.
4. Incomplete mandatory items: trial registration, data sharing statement, COI disclosures, protocol submission, or REMARK compliance for biomarker work.
5. Noncompliance with strict word, abstract, and figure/table limits, or a missing graphical abstract.

## Cover Letter

Should include:
- The significance and uniqueness of the work.
- Disclosure of any similar prior work by the authors, with copies uploaded as supplemental files and a justification of non-overlap.
- Any prior correspondence with the journal about the manuscript.
- Confirmation of originality and that the manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.
- A statement if the manuscript may be suitable for the Rapid Review program.
- For simultaneous publication with a meeting presentation, a presubmission inquiry at least two months before the meeting.

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy

- **Requirement level:** Conditional disclosure required; certain uses are forbidden.
- **Permitted scope:** Grammar, spelling, formatting, and reference cleanup need no disclosure. AI used to analyze data must be disclosed. AI writing assistance is permitted only for Original Reports and Clinical Trial Updates. AI is not permitted for opinion pieces (Art of Oncology, Comments and Controversies), Editorials, Oncology Grand Rounds, Understanding the Pathway, or targeted Reviews, and reviewers may not use AI.
- **Disclosure location:** Methods for data-analysis use; Acknowledgments for writing assistance. Disclose tool name, version, date accessed, manufacturer, and how it was used.
- **AI-generated images:** Not addressed in the JCO policy.
- **Policy URL:** https://ascopubs.org/doi/full/10.1200/JCO.23.00819

## Positioning

Submit here when the work is definitive and practice-informing: randomized phase III results, large well-powered comparative studies, pivotal biomarker analyses tied to clinical decisions, or major health-services evidence affecting cancer care delivery.

Do not submit here for single-center retrospective series, hypothesis-generating correlative analyses, or incremental real-world descriptions; those face a desk-reject ceiling on importance. A presubmission inquiry is worthwhile for time-sensitive or high-profile work.
