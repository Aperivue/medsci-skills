# Journal Profile: ESMO Open

## Journal Identity

- **Full name**: ESMO Open
- **Abbreviation**: ESMO Open
- **Publisher**: Elsevier, on behalf of the European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO)
- **ISSN**: 2059-7029 (online only)
- **Frequency**: Online-only with continuous publication
- **Impact Factor**: 10.6 (JCR 2025, as displayed on the journal homepage; confirm current value)
- **Open Access**: Full OA under CC BY-NC-ND; APC listed as USD 4,320 excluding taxes with an ESMO member discount (confirm current amount)
- **Acceptance rate**: Not published by the journal
- **Peer review**: Single blind, typically with two external reviewers

## Abstract Requirements

Original Articles require a structured abstract of up to 300 words. The guide does not name the headings; use Background, Patients and Methods, Results, Conclusions as the conventional ESMO structure and confirm at submission.

- The clinical trial registration number must appear at the end of the abstract for trials.
- Highlights are optional: 3 to 5 items, each at most 125 characters including spaces, covering only core results.

---

## Required Sections (Original Article)

1. **Title**
2. **Abstract** (structured, up to 300 words)
3. **Introduction**
4. **Material and Methods**
5. **Results**
6. **Discussion** (a combined Results and Discussion is acceptable)
7. **Conclusions** (separate section or a subsection)
8. **References**
9. **Tables and figures**

Mandatory statements:
- Funding, identifying the source and the sponsor's role in design, data collection and analysis, writing, and the decision to submit.
- Conflict of interest / competing interests, required even when there are none.
- Ethics approval with date and reference number, or a justified statement that it was not required, plus informed consent and Declaration of Helsinki compliance.
- Clinical trial registration at or before the start of enrollment, with the CONSORT checklist and flow diagram at submission; purely observational studies are exempt.
- A data availability statement is encouraged.

---

## Statistical Reporting

- Statistical formatting requirements (p-values, confidence intervals, effect sizes, software) are not itemized in the guide; report exact p-values, 95% CIs, effect sizes, and software with version as the default.
- CONSORT checklist and flow diagram are required for clinical trials.
- Real-world data studies should follow the ESMO-GROW guidance and submit its checklist.
- Sex and gender dimensions should be integrated per SAGER guidance or discussed as a limitation.
- The journal follows ICMJE and COPE guidance.

---

## Figures

- **Maximum**: Varies by article type; up to 5 figures and tables for Original Articles.
- **Resolution**: Not itemized in the guide; use uniform lettering, embed fonts, and size figures close to the intended published dimensions.
- **Format**: EPS or PDF preferred for vector drawings; TIFF or JPEG for halftones; MS Office files accepted. Avoid GIF, BMP, and screen-optimized files.
- **Submission**: Each figure as a separate file, not embedded in the manuscript; captions supplied separately with a brief title and description.
- **Color**: Color is encouraged and appears online at no additional charge; ensure accessibility for impaired color vision.
- **Graphical abstract** (optional): minimum 531 x 1328 pixels, readable at 5 x 13 cm, in TIFF, EPS, PDF, or MS Office format.

## Common Rejection Reasons

_Editorial-risk summary drawn from the official guidance and stated editorial priorities._

1. Limited novelty or clinical relevance relative to the journal's focus on innovative clinical and translational research.
2. Poor scope fit, including work better suited to a specialty or basic-science venue.
3. Missing mandatory statements: ethics approval, funding, competing interests, or trial registration.
4. Clinical trials submitted without the CONSORT checklist and flow diagram, or real-world evidence without ESMO-GROW materials.
5. Exceeding article-type limits on words, references, or figures and tables.

---

## Cover Letter

Should include, in under one page:
- What is new in the work.
- Why it fits ESMO Open's scope.
- Confirmation that the work is original and not under consideration elsewhere.

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy

- **Requirement level:** Mandatory disclosure when generative AI or AI-assisted technologies are used in the writing process.
- **Permitted scope:** Only to improve readability and language. AI must not be used to summarize or interpret data, draw scientific insights or conclusions, or make recommendations. Human oversight is mandatory and authors take full responsibility.
- **Disclosure location:** A dedicated section at the end of the manuscript before the references, titled "Declaration of Generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process". No declaration is needed for basic grammar, spelling, or reference checking tools.
- **AI-generated images:** Not addressed in the guide for authors.
- **Policy URL:** https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals

## Positioning

Submit here for solid clinical and translational oncology work that needs rapid, fully open-access dissemination: clinical trial results that are important but not flagship-defining, real-world evidence with an ESMO-relevant message, biomarker or treatment-outcome analyses, and supportive-care or practice-relevant studies.

It is also the natural transfer destination after an Annals of Oncology decision, since the Annals reviewer comments carry over. The assessment is independent and an APC applies, unlike Annals. Do not choose ESMO Open when publication cost is a hard constraint and a subscription venue would serve equally well.

| Dimension | ESMO Open | Annals of Oncology | Cancer Research and Treatment |
|-----------|-----------|--------------------|-------------------------------|
| Society | ESMO | ESMO and JSMO | Korean Cancer Association, KSMO, KALC |
| Scope | Innovative clinical and translational oncology, all disciplines | Clinically oriented oncology of particular significance | Experimental and clinical oncology across cancer types |
| Impact factor | 10.6 (JCR 2025) | 80.4 (JCR 2025 release) | See journal homepage |
| Emphasis | Fast continuous open access, Annals transfer route, APC applies | Highest clinical significance bar, hybrid access | Regional strength with international relevance, lower APC |
