# Circulation (AHA) — Detailed Profile

## 1. Basic Information
- **Journal:** Circulation
- **Publisher:** American Heart Association (Wolters Kluwer / Lippincott Williams & Wilkins)
- **Society:** American Heart Association (AHA)
- **Editor-in-Chief:** Bradley A. Maron, MD
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Scope:** Clinical cardiology, translational, and population-health cardiovascular research; see §3
- **Open Access:** Hybrid (subscription + optional Gold OA; APC per the AHA Open Access Information page — verify at submission)
- **Language:** English
- **Submission Email:** circ@circulationjournal.org
- **Print ISSN:** 0009-7322
- **Online ISSN:** 1524-4539
- **Rejection rate:** ~92% (acceptance rate 8%, AHA)
- **Impact Factor:** 41.3 (2025 JIF, Clarivate 2026 JCR release)
- **First decision:** 17 days from submission (Original Research Articles, AHA)
- **Submission system:** https://circ-submit.aha-journals.org

## 3. Scope
Circulation publishes high-quality, rigorous research aimed at improving the health of individuals and populations at risk for, or living with, cardiovascular disease. Coverage spans basic, translational, clinical, and population-health research, including clinical trials, population science, outcomes research, health policy, and community-engaged research. All submissions must clearly articulate their relevance to cardiovascular disease and highlight a message that appeals to a broad clinical and scientific readership. Manuscripts unlikely to receive sufficient priority are returned after initial screening without external review. Editors may refer manuscripts to another AHA journal before or after peer review when the fit is better elsewhere. Circulation publishes the AHA/ACC clinical practice guidelines.

## 4. Domain-Specific Outcome Measures
Clinical trials should pre-specify a primary endpoint tied to patient outcomes (e.g. major adverse cardiovascular events, mortality, hospitalization) with a pre-registered analysis plan; surrogate endpoints alone rarely suffice without a clear link to clinical outcomes. Observational and population-health work is judged on confounding control and generalizability; single-center studies need a clear case for broader relevance. Patient-reported outcomes and implementation measures strengthen population-health submissions.

## 5. Required Sections
- **Title page** with full author list, affiliations, and corresponding-author contact.
- **Abstract:** structured (Background / Methods / Results / Conclusions, ≤300 words) for Original Research; unstructured (≤350 words) for State-of-the-Art Reviews and (≤200 words) for Primers; no abstract for Research Letters, Data Briefs, Images, and most short formats.
- **Clinical Perspective** (Original Research): "What Is New?" (2–3 bullets) and "What Are the Clinical Implications?" (2–3 bullets), ≤100 words each.
- **Main text:** Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusions.
- **Data Availability Statement:** required for original research articles.
- **Disclosures / Conflicts of Interest** and **Sources of Funding** for all authors.
- **Acknowledgments**, including any generative-AI tools used (see §11).
- **Supplemental Material** as needed; brief supplemental methods are permitted for Research Letters.
- **References** (numbered, see §6), figure legends, and tables.
- No cover letter is listed among the items to gather before submission; a cover letter is mentioned for author-list changes at revision.

## 6. Reference Style
Numbered (Vancouver-style) system: references are cited in order of appearance with superscript Arabic numerals and listed in numerical order at the end of the manuscript. Journal titles are abbreviated per the NLM catalog. Include a DOI where available.

## 7. Statistical Reporting Expectations
- Pre-specified statistical analysis plan; report effect sizes with confidence intervals, not p-values alone.
- Clinical trials: register in a public trials registry (e.g. ClinicalTrials.gov) before enrollment, per ICMJE; include a CONSORT flow diagram for randomized trials.
- Observational studies: follow STROBE; address confounding with multivariable adjustment, propensity methods, or sensitivity analyses.
- Pre-specify handling of multiplicity for multiple comparisons and secondary endpoints.
- Manuscripts are subject to statistical review when appropriate.

## 8. Revision Process
- Revision window: 90 days for Original Research Articles and Research Letters.
- Respond point by point to reviewers and editors; submit the revised manuscript through the submission portal.
- Acceptance to Publish Ahead of Print: 7–10 days; acceptance to print publication: 81 days.
- A cover letter is needed for author-list changes at revision.
- Transfer: editors may offer transfer to another AHA journal (e.g. Circulation Research, JAHA) for work that fits better elsewhere.

## 9. Common Rejection Reasons
- No clear, practice-changing clinical implication — the work does not answer "does this change clinical cardiology practice?"
- Incremental or underpowered work; single-center studies without generalizability.
- Mechanistic or basic-science focus better suited to Circulation Research.
- Missing or weak Clinical Perspective.
- Scope mismatch — work better suited to a subspecialty AHA journal.
- Methods or statistics concerns; unregistered trials; incomplete reporting.

## 11. AI Writing Disclosure Policy
AHA-wide policy (shared with Stroke; same publisher): generative AI tools are permitted with documentation; AI cannot be listed as an author (ICMJE); list the tools used in Acknowledgments; describe AI use in Methods if used in research design, analysis, or figure preparation; grammar and spell-check use is exempt from disclosure; AI-generated or AI-manipulated images are prohibited; the corresponding author is responsible for the accuracy of all content, including AI-assisted text.

## 12. Positioning
- The AHA flagship journal and one of the top three cardiovascular journals globally, alongside JACC and the European Heart Journal.
- Choose Circulation over Circulation Research for clinical or translational work with direct practice implications; choose Circulation Research for mechanistic/basic science.
- Choose Circulation over JACC or the European Heart Journal when the AHA audience, guideline ecosystem, and US practice relevance fit the work.
- Publishes the AHA/ACC clinical practice guidelines that define cardiovascular care worldwide.
