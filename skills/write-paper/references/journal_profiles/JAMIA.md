# Journal Profile: Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association

## Journal Identity

- **Full name**: Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association
- **Abbreviation**: J Am Med Inform Assoc (JAMIA)
- **Publisher**: Oxford University Press, on behalf of the American Medical Informatics Association
- **ISSN**: 1067-5027 (print), 1527-974X (online)
- **Frequency**: Monthly (one volume per year, 12 issues)
- **Impact Factor**: 7.1 (JCR 2025)
- **Open Access**: Hybrid — authors may pay an open-access charge for immediate open access; otherwise standard subscription-based access with no charge
- **Acceptance rate**: Not published in the author guidelines
- **Peer review**: Not specified in the author guidelines (the title page is submitted separately from the main document)

## Abstract Requirements

Structured abstracts; no references in abstracts.

- **Research and Applications**: headings Objective, Materials and Methods, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion; up to 250 words.
- **Reviews**: a systematic review follows the same format as Research and Applications; a tutorial uses the headings Objectives, Target Audience, and Scope (covered topics); up to 250 words.
- **Brief Communications, Case Reports, Perspectives**: structured abstract, up to 150 words (the guidelines do not list separate headings for these types).
- **Correspondence, Editorials and Highlights**: no abstract requirement is stated in the guidelines.

---

## Required Sections (Original Article)

1. **Title page** — title; corresponding author's full name, postal address, e-mail, and telephone number; all co-authors' full name, department, institution, city, country, and degree; up to five keywords or phrases (MeSH terms recommended); word count excluding title page, abstract, references, figures, and tables.
2. **Abstract** — structured, as above.
3. **Main text** — sections corresponding to the abstract headings, plus a section describing Background and Significance (Research and Applications).
4. **Acknowledgments, Competing Interests, Funding, and all other required statements**.
5. **Data Availability Statement** — required for articles published in JAMIA; describe and provide means of access, where possible by linking to the data or providing a unique identifier.
6. **Reference list** — numbered consecutively in order of mention in the text; only papers published or in press.
7. **Tables** — in Word format, placed in the main text where first cited, cited in numerical order.
8. **Figure legends** — at the end of the manuscript; images uploaded as separate files and cited in numerical order.
9. **Appendices** — uploaded as Supplementary File and cited in the main text.

Manuscript order: title page, abstract, main text, tables (where first cited), Acknowledgments / Competing Interests / Funding statements, reference list, figure legends. The manuscript must be submitted as a Word document (compiled PDF accepted only if the source file is LaTeX).

## Figures

- **Maximum**: Research and Applications and Reviews — up to 6 figures and 4 tables; Brief Communications, Case Reports, Perspectives — up to 3 figures and 2 tables; Correspondence and Editorials/Highlights — up to 1 figure and 1 table.
- **Resolution**: No DPI minimum is stated in the guidelines text consulted; figure preparation follows OUP's Author Resource Centre on figures.
- **Format**: Images uploaded as separate files and cited in the main text in numerical order; legends at the end of the manuscript.
- **Color**: No color policy is stated in the guidelines text consulted.
- **Accessibility**: Alt text is required for all images, figures, illustrations, and photographs, included in the main manuscript file directly under the figure legend for the relevant figure, preceded by 'Alt text:'.
- **Tables**: Must be self-explanatory and must not duplicate the text or figures; tables longer or larger than 2 pages are published as online-only supplementary material.

## Common Rejection Reasons

1. Out of scope — the manuscript is not in biomedical or health informatics (clinical care, clinical research, translational bioinformatics, consumer health, public health, imaging, implementation, education, or policy).
2. Undisclosed overlap — related published materials (including conference-proceedings articles) not submitted for reviewers to assess the degree of overlap.
3. Reference-order errors — references not numbered sequentially as they appear in the text; the guidelines warn the manuscript may be returned for amendment before it reaches the Editor.
4. Missing required statements — no Data Availability Statement (a requirement for publication), or missing Competing Interests / Funding statements.
5. Format failures — manuscript not double-spaced, or not submitted as a Word document (PDF accepted only with a LaTeX source).
6. Undisclosed AI use — the journal screens author lists for AI tools and requires AI use to be disclosed in the cover letter and in the Methods or Acknowledgements section.

---

## Cover Letter

Should include:
- Any special considerations regarding the submission, including details of related papers by the same author(s) already published or under consideration for publication.
- Details of previous reviews of the submitted article.
- Copies of related papers, previous Editors' and reviewers' comments, and responses to those comments (submitted as "Supplementary file for Editors only").
- Disclosure of any AI use (AI use must also be disclosed in the Methods or Acknowledgements section of the manuscript).

---

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy

- **Requirement level:** Required — disclosure of AI use, in two places (see below).
- **Permitted scope:** AI use to help generate content or images, write code, process data, or for translation, with disclosure as below. AI tools do not qualify as authors, and the journal screens author lists for them.
- **Disclosure location:** Cover letter AND the Methods or Acknowledgements section of the manuscript.
- **AI-generated images:** Not banned as a separate category — use of AI to help generate images falls under the same dual-disclosure requirement above.
- **Policy URL:** https://academic.oup.com/jamia/pages/General_Instructions (no dedicated AI policy page; the guidelines point to the COPE position statement on Authorship and AI)

## Positioning

Submit here when the contribution is the informatics itself: a new method, a system or application and its evaluation, an implementation experience, or an informatics policy or education piece, aimed at AMIA's biomedical and health informatics readership. Do not submit here when the paper's contribution is a clinical finding with no informatics component, or a general biomedical study that only happens to use software — those belong in a clinical or domain journal.

| Dimension | JAMIA | Journal of Biomedical Informatics | npj Digital Medicine |
|-----------|-------|-----------------------------------|----------------------|
| Society | American Medical Informatics Association (AMIA) | None stated in profile (Elsevier) | Nature Portfolio (Springer Nature) |
| Scope | Full spectrum of biomedical and health informatics: clinical care, clinical research, translational science, implementation, imaging, education, consumer health, public health, policy | Computational and informatics methods applied to biomedical data, with emphasis on structured clinical data (EHR, claims, lab results) rather than imaging | Digital health technologies and their clinical application, Nature-family format |
