# Journal Profile: JAMA Surgery

## Journal Identity

- **Full name**: JAMA Surgery
- **Abbreviation**: JAMA Surg
- **Publisher**: American Medical Association (AMA), JAMA Network
- **ISSN**: 2168-6254 (print), 2168-6262 (online)
- **Frequency**: "published online weekly, every Wednesday, and in print/online issues 12 times a year"
- **Impact Factor**: 15.6 (stated on the journal's For Authors page: "The Journal Impact Factor is 15.6"; JCR year not stated there)
- **Open Access**: Hybrid — "Without any author fees, all research articles are made free access online 12 months after publication"; authors may pay for immediate open access (fee not stated on the journal's public pages)
- **Acceptance rate**: 9% ("The journal's acceptance rate is 9%.")
- **Peer review**: Single-anonymized ("peer reviewer identities are kept confidential" and "author identities are made known to reviewers"); median time to first decision 11 days, 41 days with review

## Manuscript Types and Word Limits

| Type | Body Word Limit | Abstract | References | Figures/Tables |
|------|----------------|----------|------------|----------------|
| Original Investigation | 3000 words | 350 words (structured) | 50-75 (per study type) | 5 total |
| Research Letter | 800 words | None | 10 | 2 small |
| Systematic Review (without meta-analysis) | 3000 words | 350 words (structured) | 50-75 | 5 |
| Meta-analysis | 3000 words | 350 words (structured) | 50-75 | 5 |
| Narrative Review | 2000-3500 words (maximum) | 300 words (3-part structured) | 50-75 | 5 |
| Viewpoint | 1200 words (1000 with 1 small table/figure) | None | 7 | 1 small (optional) |
| Letter to the Editor | 400 words | None | 5 | Not stated on the journal's public pages |
| Letter in Reply | 500 words | None | 6 | Not stated on the journal's public pages |

Original Investigation word count is text only ("Maximum length: 3000 words of text"), excluding abstract, tables, figures, acknowledgments, references and online-only material. Systematic and Narrative Reviews require a presubmission inquiry. Viewpoints are limited to 4 authors; letters to 3 authors. Letters to the Editor must be submitted within 4 weeks of the article's online publication. Special Communication is listed as an article category; its limits were not located on the journal's public pages.

## Abstract Requirements

Original Investigation: "an abstract of no more than 350 words", structured:

```
Importance:
Objective:
Design, Setting, and Participants:
Intervention(s) (clinical trials) or Exposure(s) (observational studies):
Main Outcome(s) and Measure(s):
Results:
Conclusions and Relevance:
Trial Registration: (clinical trials only)
```

Systematic Review: "a structured abstract of no more than 350 words" — Importance, Objective, Evidence Review, Findings, Conclusions and Relevance.

Narrative Review: "a 3-part structured abstract of no more than 300 words" — Importance, Observations, Conclusions and Relevance.

## Required Sections (Original Article)

1. **Key Points** — before the Abstract; Question (1 sentence), Findings (1-2 sentences, basic numbers and whether significant, no test statistics), Meaning (1 sentence); 75-100 words total; required for research and review manuscripts (not Narrative Reviews)
2. **Abstract** (structured, above)
3. **Introduction**
4. **Methods**
   - Study type stated (required for Original Investigation)
   - EQUATOR reporting guideline for the study type
   - For randomized trials: protocol with complete statistical analysis plan cited in Methods and submitted as a supplement; power or sample size statement
5. **Results**
6. **Discussion**
7. **Data Sharing Statement** (required for Original Investigation)
8. **Acknowledgment** — including any AI use in manuscript preparation

Study types and reporting guidelines listed by the journal: randomized clinical trials (CONSORT), nonrandomized trials (TREND), meta-analysis (PRISMA or MOOSE), cohort / case-control / cross-sectional (STROBE), economic evaluation and decision analytical model (CHEERS), comparative effectiveness (ISPOR), genetic association (STREGA), diagnostic/prognostic (STARD or TRIPOD), quality improvement (SQUIRE), survey (AAPOR; response rates generally expected to be at least 60%), qualitative (SRQR or COREQ), case series.

## Statistical Reporting

- "P values should never be presented alone without the data that are being compared"; give them after the compared absolute numbers or rates and measures of uncertainty (e.g., "0.8%, 95% CI −0.2% to 1.8%; P = .13"); values below .001 as "P<.001"
- Report numerical results with indicators of uncertainty such as confidence intervals
- Include absolute numbers or rates alongside ratio measures
- State any a priori significance level and whether tests were 1- or 2-sided
- Means with SDs for normally distributed data; medians with ranges or IQRs otherwise
- Randomized trials: intention-to-treat results; "a statement of the power or sample size calculation is required"
- Diagnostic studies: sensitivity, specificity and likelihood ratios
- Surveys: response or participation rates
- "include the statistical software used to perform the analysis, including the version and manufacturer", plus any extension packages

## Figures

- **Maximum**: 5 tables and/or figures combined for Original Investigation
- **Resolution**: "≥350 ppi" for photographs and illustrations; "≥600 ppi" for line drawings
- **Format**: photographs and clinical images at initial submission ".eps, .jpg, .pdf, .ppt, .psd, .tif"; graphs at initial submission .ai, .bmp, .docx, .emf, .eps, .jpg, .pdf, .ppt, .psd, .tif, .wmf or .xls, and at revision and publication .ai, .emf, .eps, .pdf, .wmf or .xls; each main-text figure uploaded as a separate file
- **File size**: "We recommend individual file sizes of no more than 500 kB and not exceeding 1 MB, with the total size for all files not exceeding 5 MB (not including any video files)"
- **Color**: RGB mode with Adobe RGB or sRGB profile
- **AI**: clinical images or illustrations created or manipulated by AI are not permitted unless part of a formal, disclosed research design; legends of any AI-generated figures must note it

## Common Rejection Reasons

Drawn from the journal's stated requirements and 9% acceptance rate, not from confidential review:

1. **Missing required components** — Key Points, study type, data sharing statement or EQUATOR checklist absent from a research manuscript
2. **Over length** — more than 3000 words of text or more than 5 tables/figures combined in an Original Investigation
3. **Review submitted without a presubmission inquiry** — required for Systematic and Narrative Reviews
4. **P values reported alone** — without the compared data and confidence intervals
5. **Survey with a low response rate** — the journal generally expects at least 60%
6. **AI misuse** — AI-generated references, AI-drafted opinion pieces or letters, or AI-created clinical images

Should include:

- Whether the authors have published, posted or submitted any related papers from the same study

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy

- **Requirement level:** Required
- **Permitted scope:** AI may assist manuscript preparation, but authors "must review and confirm the accuracy of all content generated by artificial intelligence (AI)" and are accountable for it. AI "should not be used to generate or format references", and "Use of AI, LLM, and chatbots to draft Opinion manuscripts" (and letters) is not permitted. Nonhuman AI tools "do not qualify for authorship".
- **Disclosure location:** Acknowledgment section for content creation, revision or formatting, giving "a description of the content that was created or edited", the tool's name, version and extension numbers, manufacturer, "date(s) of use", and confirmation that the authors take responsibility for the integrity of the content generated; Methods when AI is part of the research design (with a data sharing statement covering code)
- **AI-generated images:** Not permitted for clinical images and clinical illustrations unless part of a formal research design and fully disclosed
- **Policy URL:** https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamasurgery/pages/instructions-for-authors

## Positioning

Submit here for surgical research with a clear clinical message for practicing surgeons: trials, large outcomes and quality-improvement studies, comparative effectiveness and cost-effectiveness analyses, and studies of surgical workforce, education or emerging technology. The journal states its mission is "to promote the art and science of surgery" and that it is the official publication of the Association of VA Surgeons, the Pacific Coast Surgical Association, and the Surgical Outcomes Club.

Do not prioritize JAMA Surgery for work whose main audience is not surgeons (send to JAMA or JAMA Network Open), for a single-subspecialty technical question better read by that subspecialty, or when the manuscript cannot fit 3000 words and 5 tables/figures without losing its core results.
