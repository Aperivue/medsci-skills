# Journal Profile: European Heart Journal

## Journal Identity

- **Full name**: European Heart Journal
- **Abbreviation**: Eur Heart J
- **Publisher**: Oxford University Press (on behalf of the European Society of Cardiology, ESC)
- **ISSN**: 0195-668X (print), 1522-9645 (online)
- **Frequency**: 48 issues a year
- **Impact Factor**: 43.5 (2025 Journal Impact Factor; journal's OUP About page; rank 2/237 in Cardiac & Cardiovascular Systems; 5-year Impact Factor 41.5; CiteScore 35.9)
- **Open Access**: Hybrid — subscription journal with optional open access; no charge under a standard licence (exclusive licence to the ESC); open access carries a charge, including for invited manuscripts; OUP Read and Publish agreements cover the charge for authors at participating institutions
- **Peer review**: Single-anonymised (single-blind) — author identities known to editors and reviewers, reviewer identities hidden from authors; Editorial Board triage first (manuscripts may be returned without external review for insufficient priority or better fit to another ESC journal); typically at least two reviewers; detailed statistical review for provisionally accepted papers; Editor-in-Chief makes the final decision; Fast Track initial decision within 7 working days for selected papers

## Abstract Requirements

Text abstract for most article types, submitted as a separate file in Editorial Manager at initial submission.

```
Background and Aims:
Methods:
Results:
Conclusions:
```

- **Clinical Research Article, Translational Science Article, Meta-analysis**: structured text abstract with the headings above; 250 words maximum; no references. Plus a Structured Graphical Abstract (below).
- **State of the Art Review, Hypothesis, Great Debate, Special Article**: unstructured text abstract; 250 words maximum; no references or abbreviations. Plus a Graphical Abstract.
- **Rapid Communications, Discussion Forum, Cardiovascular Flashlight, CardioPulse**: no abstract.
- **Viewpoint, Editorial**: graphical abstract only, no text abstract.
- **Structured Graphical Abstract** (required for Clinical Research Article, Translational Science Article, Meta-analysis): two parts submitted as separate files — (1) text of up to 40 words under each of three headings: Key Question, Key Finding, Take-home Message; (2) graphic, 11.0 cm x 18.0 cm landscape, minimum 300 ppi, sans-serif font 10-12 points (no smaller than 8), vector file (.pdf, .ai, .svg) for graphs or high-resolution (300 ppi) .tiff for photographs, legend of up to 80 words. Accepted graphical abstracts are professionally redrawn by the journal's illustrators.
- **Graphical Abstract** (required for State of the Art Review, Editorial, Hypothesis, Viewpoint, Special Article): visual summary of the main (take-home) messages; same 11.0 cm x 18.0 cm landscape size, 300 ppi minimum, sans-serif 10-12 point font, legend up to 80 words.
- **Translational Perspective** (required for Translational Science Article only): summary of relevant translational aspects for clinical applications; 100 words maximum; no references or abbreviations.
- **Audio Abstract** (recommended for Clinical Research Article, Translational Science Article, Meta-analysis, State of the Art Review, Hypothesis, Viewpoint): audio summary of the take-home messages; authors supply the text in a separate file and a professional speaker records it if approved.
- **Summary**: authors also give an outline of the paper in a Summary for the editorial team to evaluate and process the paper; unlike abstracts, summaries are for internal use only and are not published.

## Required Sections (Original Article)

Clinical Research Articles and Translational Science Articles should be structured as follows:

1. **Title page** — full title; all author names and affiliations; institution where the work was performed; mailing address and email address of one corresponding author. Titles should be as short as possible, carry the most important keywords first, and avoid abbreviations or acronyms unless the title would be very long without them.
2. **Text abstract and keywords** — structured, as above; up to 6 keywords.
3. **Structured graphical abstract or graphical abstract** — as above.
4. **Translational Perspective** — Translational Science Articles only.
5. **Introduction**
6. **Methods** — ethics: human experimentation must comply with the Declaration of Helsinki, protocol approved by the local ethics committee, informed consent obtained and stated; animal work follows ARRIVE; reporting follows the EQUATOR guideline for the study type (CONSORT for randomised trials, with prospective registration in a public registry such as ClinicalTrials.gov and the registration number and register name in the paper; PRISMA and MOOSE for systematic reviews and meta-analyses, registered on PROSPERO; STROBE plus CODE-EHR minimum standards for observational studies and registry work; CHEERS, STARD, STREGA, GATHER, MIAME, SPIRIT, SRQR, SQUIRE as applicable). Sex and gender reported per SAGER (titles and abstracts state the sex(es)/gender(s) the study applies to; methods state how sex/gender was considered; results stratified as appropriate, or the rationale for not analysing given); race and ethnicity per C4DISC (self-identification preferred, populations described specifically); age and nationality inclusion/stratification stated; all data de-identified.
7. **Results** — sex/gender-stratified results as appropriate; mean and median values and range for age.
8. **Discussion**
9. **Acknowledgements** — substantive contributions from individuals who do not meet ICMJE authorship criteria.
10. **Funding** — full agency names without abbreviations, complete grant numbers in brackets; NIH-funded articles are deposited in PubMed Central by OUP.
11. **Disclosure of interest** — every author submits an ICMJE disclosure form (a form is required even with no conflicts, stating "none declared"); a concise summary goes in the separate Declarations Form. Authors holding a position on the EHJ Editorial Board must declare that role.
12. **Data availability statement** — required even if no data are presented or discussed ("No new data were generated or analysed in support of this research" where applicable); deidentified data and software code on which the conclusions rely are strongly encouraged to be made available (main manuscript, supporting files, or a public repository); publicly available datasets must be fully referenced with an accession number or DOI, with a [dataset] tag at the start of the citation.
13. **References** — Vancouver style: Arabic numerals in order cited; complete information including article title, abbreviated journal title and page numbers; first 6 authors listed followed by "et al." when there are more than 6; journal titles abbreviated per MEDLINE; personal communications, manuscripts in preparation and other unpublished data are not cited in the reference list.
14. **Figure legends**
15. **Appendices**

Figures, tables and supplementary files are uploaded as separate files in Editorial Manager. Format-free initial submission is permitted (manuscript without journal-specific formatting, figures embedded or separate, page numbers included), but authors must still answer all submission-form questions, provide a completed Declarations Form, and submit all manuscript parts including the abstract, figures, figure legends and supplementary files; a revised manuscript must follow Manuscript Preparation (Word .doc, double-spaced, numbered pages, line numbers restarting on each page, Oxford English spelling, SI units, numbers one to ten written out in full). Manuscripts must be original, not under consideration elsewhere, and contain nothing abusive, defamatory, libellous, obscene, fraudulent or illegal. Preprints (Author's Original Version) may be posted anywhere at any time except for ESC Guidelines; medRxiv/bioRxiv preprints can be submitted directly to EHJ from the preprint server. Contributors use the CRediT taxonomy; group authorship is permitted for legitimate groups whose members collectively meet ICMJE criteria.

## Statistical Reporting

The guidelines state:

- Adequate statistical methods are required for publication; authors should be "as simple as possible, but as sophisticated as needed" — clinical trials with their formalized framework must meet more specific statistical standards than pathophysiological studies.
- Include absolute risk as well as relative risk where possible.
- For studies with a sophisticated design, collaboration with a professional statistician is recommended.
- As part of review, the manuscript may be sent to a Statistical Reviewer; all provisionally accepted papers undergo a detailed statistical review.
- Authors should refer to "Statistical Analysis" in the EHJ Quality Standards document and to the journal's detailed summary of study design, analysis and reporting points before submitting.
- Figure legends should give the exact P-value where possible (e.g. P = 0.003) and, for graphs, the number of animals/cells/observations and the statistical test applied.

## Figures

- **Maximum**: typically no more than 8 figures and tables in total; research article types have no maximum number of figures or tables, but submissions should typically have no more than 8 in total; no more than 6 panels per figure. Figures must not duplicate data from tables or text.
- **Resolution**: photographs and images minimum 350 dpi (recommended 600 dpi); line art minimum 600 dpi (recommended 1200 dpi); structured graphical abstract graphic minimum 300 ppi. It is not possible to improve image quality during production.
- **Format**: charts, graphs and diagrams as vector graphics (.eps, .svg, .ai, .pdf); photographs as raster (.tif/.tiff or .raw); videos .mp4 or .avi only, each with a still image (.eps, .svg, .ai or .pdf). Each figure is a separate file; multipanel figures are submitted as a single file with panels labelled A, B, C in the upper-left corner, combined with dedicated software (not PowerPoint).
- **Color**: colour preferred for charts, graphs and diagrams; CMYK or RGB; avoid pale colours and red/green combinations that are hard for colour-blind readers. No charge for colour figures of any type.
- **Font**: sans-serif (Myriad Pro, Arial, or Helvetica) in figures; line-art text no smaller than 2 mm in height.
- **Tables**: portrait layout preferred, no more than 9 columns, editable text (never embedded as an image), numbered and referenced in the text, not split into parts; each table uploaded as a separate file.
- **Legends**: in a separate "Legends" section after the References; define all symbols and abbreviations; for graphs include the number of animals/cells/observations and the statistical test, with the exact P-value where possible.
- **Accessibility**: alt text is required for all images, figures, illustrations and photographs, included in the main manuscript file directly under the figure caption, preceded by "Alt text:".
- **Rules**: permission must be sought and granted for any reproduced figure, table or video, with full attribution in the legend; do not submit figures whose copyright is unclear. Supplementary figures, tables and videos are online-only and must be submitted with the original manuscript.

## Common Rejection Reasons

1. Insufficient priority at Editorial Board triage — manuscripts are returned without external review if they are deemed more appropriate for another ESC journal, do not meet submission requirements, or have insufficient priority for further consideration.
2. Wrong lane — single-centre observational studies with limited generalizability, and preclinical work without a clear clinical implication, are poor fits for the flagship; the journal offers transfer to another ESC journal instead.
3. Missing required elements — no text abstract, no structured graphical abstract, no Declarations Form, no Data Availability Statement, no CRediT roles or ICMJE conflict-of-interest forms, or (for trials) no prospective registration number.
4. Reporting-guideline non-compliance — CONSORT for trials, PRISMA/MOOSE for meta-analyses, STROBE for observational studies, and other EQUATOR checklists not followed; trial registration missing.
5. Undisclosed or impermissible AI use — AI tools do not qualify as authors and the journal screens author lists for them; undisclosed AI use in content, images, code, data processing or translation breaches the disclosure rule.
6. Statistical inadequacy — methods not rigorous or not matched to the design; provisionally accepted papers face a detailed statistical review.
7. Prior or duplicate publication, plagiarism or image manipulation — manuscripts are screened with iThenticate and third-party integrity services; manuscripts must be original and not under consideration elsewhere.

## Cover Letter

The guidelines consulted state:

- No cover letter is listed among the requirements for a regular initial submission (answer all submission-form questions, provide a completed Declarations Form, submit all manuscript parts).
- A cover letter IS required for a Fast Track petition, explaining why the manuscript is suitable for Fast Track status, submitted with the text abstract and the manuscript as a "Fast Track Petition" article type.
- The corresponding author should indicate in the cover letter if an Editor from EHJ is a co-author or contributor to the manuscript.
- Authors should include a note in the cover letter at submission if an embargo is required, stating the exact date and time the paper can be published.
- AI use must be disclosed in a cover letter at the point of submission (see AI Writing Disclosure Policy).

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy

- **Requirement level:** Required — "The use of AI (for example, to help generate content or images, write code, process data, or for translation) should be disclosed in a cover letter at the point of submission and explained in full in a Methods or Acknowledgements section in the manuscript."
- **Permitted scope:** AI may assist with generating content or images, writing code, processing data, or translation, if disclosed as above; authors remain responsible for the content. AI tools (natural language processing tools driven by AI) do not qualify as authors, and the Journal will screen for them in author lists. See the COPE position statement on Authorship and AI for more detail.
- **Disclosure location:** Cover letter at submission, plus a full explanation in the Methods or Acknowledgements section of the manuscript.
- **AI-generated images:** No separate ban is stated; AI-generated images fall under the same disclosure rule as other AI use (cover letter plus Methods or Acknowledgements).
- **Policy URL:** https://academic.oup.com/EURHEARTJ/pages/General_Instructions (section "Artificial Intelligence" under Publication Ethics)

## Positioning

Submit here for innovative clinical or translational cardiovascular research that can change practice or inform ESC guidelines: randomized and nonrandomized trials, large cohort and registry studies, meta-analyses on controversial topics, and ESC Guidelines and official output, for a readership of cardiologists and cardiovascular researchers worldwide. The mandatory structured graphical abstract and 250-word structured abstract force a crisp claim, and Fast Track review suits congress-timed work. Do not submit purely mechanistic basic science without a clinical implication, single-centre observational work with limited generalizability, or case reports (the image-only Cardiovascular Flashlight, 250 words, is the only case-based type); those belong in a basic-science journal or an ESC specialty journal, to which EHJ can transfer the paper with its reviews.
