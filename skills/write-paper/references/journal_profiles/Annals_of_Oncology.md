# Journal Profile: Annals of Oncology

## Journal Identity

- **Full name**: Annals of Oncology
- **Abbreviation**: Ann Oncol
- **Publisher**: Elsevier, on behalf of the European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO) and the Japanese Society of Medical Oncology (JSMO)
- **ISSN**: 0923-7534 (print), 1569-8041 (online)
- **Frequency**: Monthly, 12 issues per year
- **Impact Factor**: 80.4 (JCR 2025 release, as reported on the journal homepage; confirm current value)
- **Open Access**: Hybrid; no page charges
- **Acceptance rate**: Not published by the journal
- **Peer review**: Editorial desk review aiming to return low-priority papers within 3 weeks; external review for high-priority papers; author notification within 6 weeks

## Manuscript Types and Word Limits

| Type | Body Word Limit | Abstract | References | Figures/Tables |
|------|----------------|----------|------------|----------------|
| Original Article | 4,500 (excluding heading, abstract, acknowledgements, funding, references) | Structured, 300 words or fewer | Unrestricted, not counted toward the word limit | No formal cap; no more than 6 in total recommended, each counting 150 words toward the limit |
| Review | 5,000 | Generally unstructured; systematic reviews use Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results, Conclusions | Unrestricted | No fixed limit; online-only presentation may be offered |
| In Brief Trial Update | 1,200 | Structured | Unrestricted | 3 total |
| Letter to the Editor | 500 | Not required | 5 | 1 |
| Editorial | Agreed between editor and author | Not required | Not stated | Not stated |
| Special Article | Not stated | Not stated | Not stated | Not stated |

Manuscripts exceeding limits by no more than 10% are not automatically rejected, but shortening may be required at revision.

## Abstract Requirements

Original Articles require a structured abstract of 300 words or fewer:

```text
Background
Patients and Methods
Results
Conclusions
```

- No undefined abbreviations or unspecified references.
- The clinical trial registration number must follow the abstract where applicable.

---

## Required Sections (Original Article)

1. **Title page** with title, authors, affiliations, corresponding author, word count, and running title
2. **Structured abstract**
3. **Key Message** of at most 400 characters, required for Original Articles and Reviews
4. **Introduction**
5. **Patients and Methods**
6. **Results**
7. **Discussion / Conclusions**
8. **Acknowledgements**
9. **Funding statement**, beginning "This work was supported by [...]", with full official funder names, or a statement that there was no funding
10. **Declaration of competing interests** (mandatory)
11. **References**

A submission letter must confirm that the report is not under consideration elsewhere and that all named authors agreed to submission. Clinical studies must include a statement of ethics committee approval consistent with the Declaration of Helsinki.

---

## Statistical Reporting

- Reporting-guideline compliance is mandatory where applicable, with the completed checklist submitted as a separate file and page numbers cited per item:
  - CONSORT 2010 for randomized trials, including the flow diagram and intention-to-treat reporting
  - STROBE for observational studies
  - PRISMA for systematic reviews and meta-analyses
  - REMARK for tumor marker and biomarker studies
  - TRIPOD for prediction models
  - ESMO-GROW with checklist and flowchart for real-world data studies
- Phase III trials are evaluated against CONSORT and may undergo statistical review.
- p-value format, confidence intervals, effect sizes, and software are not itemized in the guide; report exact p-values, 95% CIs, effect sizes, and software with version as the default.

---

## Figures

- **Maximum**: Not formally limited; no more than 6 figures and tables in total is recommended, and each counts as 150 words toward the 4,500-word limit.
- **Resolution**: Not stated in the guide; use 300 dpi for photographs and higher for line art as the Elsevier default.
- **Format**: TIFF, JPEG, or EPS with source files. Do not supply GIF, BMP, PICT, WPG, screen-optimized, low-resolution, or disproportionately large files.
- **Color**: Color figures appear free online regardless of print, are recolored to journal style, and must be supplied in editable format.
- **Graphical abstract** (optional): minimum 531 x 1328 pixels, readable at 5 x 13 cm, in TIFF, EPS, PDF, or MS Office format.

## Common Rejection Reasons

_Editorial-risk summary drawn from the official guidance and stated editorial priorities._

1. Insufficient priority or significance at the desk-review stage, typically returned within 3 weeks.
2. Weak clinical relevance: mechanistic studies without clear clinical translation, or biomarker work without actionable future clinical utility.
3. Limited originality or scientific quality relative to a very high acceptance bar.
4. Missing or incomplete mandatory reporting checklists, funding statement, competing-interest declaration, ethics approval, or trial registration.
5. Exceeding word limits substantially, or omitting the Key Message.

---

## Cover Letter

Should include:
- Confirmation that the report is not under consideration elsewhere and that all named authors agreed to submission.
- A brief statement of significance and clinical priority, avoiding claims about impact factor or readership size.
- Ethics, trial registration, funding, and competing-interest context where relevant.
- Consider a presubmission enquiry through the Editorial Manager database before preparing a full manuscript; it is strongly encouraged.

## Positioning

Submit here for clinically oriented oncology work of particular significance: practice-relevant trials, major translational findings with direct clinical actionability, pivotal biomarker analyses, and influential ESMO-adjacent evidence.

Do not submit here for descriptive single-center series, mechanistic biology without a clinical bridge, or biomarker work without a credible path to clinical use. Use the presubmission enquiry pathway first when priority is uncertain; if rejected, the transfer route to ESMO Open preserves reviewer comments.

| Society | ESMO and JSMO | ASCO | ESMO |
