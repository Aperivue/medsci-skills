# Journal Profile: AJNR (American Journal of Neuroradiology)

## Basic Information

- **Publisher:** American Society of Neuroradiology (ASNR)
- **Impact Factor:** Not found on the author guidelines page (checked 2026-11)
- **Scope:** Clinical imaging, therapy, and basic science of the central and peripheral nervous system, including the brain, spine, head and neck, interventional procedures, techniques in neuroimaging and neurointervention, and related educational, socioeconomic, and medicolegal issues
- **Open Access:** Not found on the author guidelines page (open-access model and fees not stated there; checked 2026-11)
- **Submission portal:** Manuscript Central
- **Peer review:** Blinded — author names appear only on the title page, submitted as a separate electronic file; the manuscript file must not mention the authors' institutions, affiliations, or previous work stated as the authors' own
- **Corresponding author:** Only one corresponding author per paper; more than one senior author can be acknowledged in the paper footnotes

---

## Scope — Covered Topics

The journal's scope statement: "articles pertaining to the clinical imaging, therapy, and basic science of the central and peripheral nervous system, including but not limited to the brain, spine, head and neck, interventional procedures, techniques in neuroimaging and neurointervention, and related educational, socioeconomic, and medicolegal issues." AJNR states that it "encourages and will give particular attention to investigations that are prospective in nature and in which specific hypotheses are proposed."

Case reports are not an AJNR article type; they go to the AJNR Case Collection (maximum 5 authors, discussion of at most 400 words, at most 3 references).

## Manuscript Types and Word Limits

The word count covers the entire article from the Introduction through the Acknowledgments, including References and Tables, but excluding the Abstract and Figure Legends. Review Articles exclude references from the word count. Figures are counted as a maximum number of figures with a maximum total number of parts across all figures.

| Type | Abstract (words) | Max Word Count | Max References | Max Tables | Max Figures (Parts) |
|------|------------------|----------------|----------------|------------|---------------------|
| Original Research | 350 | 4000* | 50 | 5 | 7 (15) |
| Systematic Reviews/Meta-Analyses | 350 | 4000* | 100 | 5 | 7 (15) |
| Review Articles | 200 | 5000, references not included* | 100 | 5 | 15 (30) |
| Clinical Reports | 250 | 3500* | 30 | 2 | 7 (15) |
| Point/Counterpoint (each side) | 150 | 2000 | 15 | 1 | 4 (8) |
| Brief/Technical Reports | 250 | 3000 | 30 | 1 | 7 (15) |
| State of Practice | 300 | 3000* | 75 | 5 | 15 (30) |
| Video Article | 150 | Not found on the author guidelines page | 20 | 0 | Video Template |
| eLetters to the Editor (Responses) | Not found on the author guidelines page | 500 | 4 | 2 | 2 (5) |
| Editorials | Not found on the author guidelines page | 500 | 4 | 1 | 1 (2) |
| Book Reviews | Not found on the author guidelines page | 500 | 4 | 1 | 1 (2) |

\*Over-length fee (word count only, and only for the article types marked \*): authors who wish to exceed the word limit are charged $850, with a maximum of 1000 words above the set limit for the article type, or $1500 for up to 1500 words above it. The reference, table, and figure limits are separate and carry no fee.

The article types AJNR lists as considered for publication are Original Research, Systematic Reviews/Meta-Analyses, Review Articles, Clinical Reports, Point/Counterpoint Articles, Brief/Technical Reports, Letters to the Editor, and State of Practice papers.

- **Systematic Reviews/Meta-Analyses** can be submitted under the "Original Research" manuscript type in Manuscript Central; they must follow the PRISMA guidelines, and authors should upload a copy of the PRISMA checklist to their Manuscript Files.
- **Review Articles:** authors are encouraged to contact the Editor-in-Chief before preparing an unsolicited review; the abstract is a one-paragraph summary.
- **Clinical Reports:** series of more than five cases, retrospective and descriptive; one-paragraph abstract.
- **Brief/Technical Reports:** unusually educational or medically important work with limited or preliminary data; not intended for case reports; limited to 2 per issue.
- **State of Practice** (formerly White Papers and Perspectives): title includes "State of Practice"; abstract of 300 words or less with Background, Methods, Key Message.

## Abstract Requirements

**Original Research — structured abstract, 350 words.** The abstract describes the essential aspects of the investigation in four subheadings:

```
Background and Purpose: [...]
Materials and Methods: [...]
Results: [summarize actual data]
Conclusions: [brief statement directed to the stated purpose or hypothesis; no references cited]
```

**Systematic Reviews/Meta-Analyses:** Background, Purpose, Data Sources, Study Selection, Data Analysis, Data Synthesis, Limitations, Conclusions.

- The abstract is included in the main document as well as in the space provided in the Corresponding Author Center.
- No abbreviations other than those on AJNR's standard abbreviation list are permitted in the abstract.

## Required Sections

Suggested Original Research outline (AJNR does not prescribe this full order; the placement rules it does state are given in the items themselves):

1. **Title page** — submitted as a separate electronic file carrying the authors' names (blinding), not part of the manuscript file. Avoid qualifying the research as a "preliminary study" in the title.
2. **Abstract** — as above; included in the main document.
3. **Introduction** — guidance (not an AJNR rule): end with the study purpose or hypothesis.
4. **Materials and Methods** — do not include manufacturers' names unless the specific product is important to the procedures performed.
5. **Results**
6. **Discussion**
7. **Conclusions** — in a separate section, summarize the major findings of the study and their clinical usefulness (if applicable).
8. **Acknowledgments** — a separate section, not necessarily a freestanding page; obtaining permission from each acknowledged individual is advised.
9. **References**
10. **Tables** — inserted into the main document after the references, each on its own page.
11. **Figure legends** — after the tables in the main document, all legends together on a single page, in the present tense.

Format: Microsoft Word (DOC, DOCX) or RTF; all text double-spaced with sufficient margins; margins not justified; all pages numbered; font at least 12 point.

**Abbreviations:** a standard list of abbreviations needs no expansion. A total of 10 other abbreviations may be used within the text, legends, and tables; these are listed in the abbreviation key and spelled out at first mention in the text. Every abbreviated term must be used at least 10 times within the manuscript.

**Reporting checklists:** AJNR recommends that Original Research and Systematic Reviews/Meta-Analyses adhere to the applicable guideline (STARD, CONSORT, PRISMA, STROBE, SPIRIT, AGREE, ARRIVE, SQUIRE, TRIPOD, SRQR, CHEERS); a checklist may be included as supplementary material. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses must follow the PRISMA guidelines, and authors should upload a copy of the PRISMA checklist.

**AI/machine learning/deep learning studies:** AJNR recommends that authors refer to its Study-Specific Guidelines for AI in Medical Imaging ("Bridging Gaps in Reference Standard and Clinical Evaluation") and report the level of evidence of the study (Levels of Evidence table, Level 1 to Level 7).

**Code:** AJNR recommends that all computer code used for modeling and/or data analysis be deposited in a publicly accessible repository no later than the time of acceptance, with a link to it in the final manuscript.

**Revisions:** submit two versions — one labeled "Revised Manuscript" with all changes visibly marked in yellow, and a clean version without highlighting, named "Clean Manuscript".

## Supplementary Material Convention

- Submit the supplemental material as one separate PDF: combine any appendix, tables, or figures into a single PDF file.
- All contents of that PDF are referred to as "Supplemental Data" within the article, not as individual elements (eg, not "Online Figure 1" or "Online Table 2").
- AJNR no longer copyedits, typesets, or proofreads supplemental material.
- Tables that would be set broadside are removed automatically and placed as supplemental material.

## Statistical Reporting

Stated by AJNR:

- Papers with statistical analysis should be prepared in consultation or coauthorship with someone who has statistical expertise.
- P values should generally be reported to two decimal places if greater than .01, three if between .001 and .01, and "<.001" otherwise.
- P values from two-sided tests, unless the design requires a one-sided test (eg, noninferiority studies).
- Measures of association, odds ratios, correlation coefficients, and diagnostic accuracy (sensitivity, specificity, etc) ordinarily to two digits past the decimal place unless the scientific context warrants greater precision.

General guidance (not AJNR requirements): 95% CI for primary and secondary outcomes; for ROC analysis, AUC with 95% CI and the test used to compare curves.

## Figure Requirements

- **Maximum:** per article type (see the table above); Original Research — 7 figures with 15 parts in total.
- **Files:** each image is a separate file with the figure number in the filename; do not insert figures into the manuscript.
- **Format:** JPG, TIF, or EPS.
- **Resolution:** minimum 4 inches wide at the stated resolution — line art (black-and-white) 1200 dpi/ppi; halftones 300 dpi/ppi; combination halftones 600 dpi/ppi.
- **Orientation:** sagittal projections or lateral images with the patient facing the reader's left.
- **Blinding:** no authors' names in the filename or in the image file itself.
- **Manipulation:** any cloning, whether to delete or enhance part of an image, is viewed as suspicious; any manipulation of an image must be explained in the legend.
- **Color:** Not found on the author guidelines page (checked 2026-11).

**Tables:** double-spaced, each beginning on a separate page, without vertical or horizontal rules; graphic files are not acceptable for tables.

## Reference Style

- Numbered consecutively in the order of appearance in the text; cited in the text in superscript; double-spaced.
- All authors listed when there are three or fewer; with four or more, the first three followed by "et al."
- Journal names abbreviated per Index Medicus.
- Example: Callen AL, Chow DS, Chen YA, et al. Predictive value of noncontrast head CT with negative findings in the emergency department setting. AJNR Am J Neuroradiol 2020;41:213–18. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3174/ajnr.A6408

## Common Rejection Reasons

AJNR's author guidelines do not list rejection reasons. The checks below are requirements stated on the guidelines page:

1. Manuscript blinding broken — author names outside the separate title-page file, or institutions, affiliations, or previous work by the authors (stated as such) mentioned in the manuscript file.
2. Figure blinding broken — authors' names in a figure filename or in the image file itself.
3. Over the word limit for the article type without accepting the over-length fee (fee available only for the types marked \* in the limits table), or over the reference, table, or figure (parts) limits, which have no fee option.
4. Non-standard abbreviations in the abstract, more than 10 non-standard abbreviations, or abbreviations used fewer than 10 times.
5. Figures embedded in the manuscript, below the stated resolution, or in a format other than JPG, TIF, or EPS.
6. Supplemental material not combined into one PDF.
7. A systematic review or meta-analysis that does not follow the PRISMA guidelines (and, as AJNR advises, authors should upload the PRISMA checklist).

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy

- **Requirement level:** Required — use of a large language model must be documented.
- **Permitted scope:** LLMs such as ChatGPT do not satisfy authorship criteria and cannot be listed as an author of a manuscript, commentary, or letter. The authors accept full responsibility for the text's factual and citation accuracy; mathematical, logical, and common-sense reasoning; and originality.
- **Disclosure location:** The Methods section or an acknowledgment at the end of the manuscript, including a description of the content created or edited, the name of the language model or tool, the version number, and the manufacturer/developer.
- **AI-generated images:** Not found on the author guidelines page; AJNR lists a separate page, "Use of Large Language Models and Other AI Tools", that was not read (checked 2026-11).
- **Policy URL:** https://www.ajnr.org/content/manuscript-submission-guidelines

- **AI-use disclosure placement**: Methods or Acknowledgments

## Positioning Strategy

Guidance, based on the two journals' own scope statements:

### Choose AJNR over JNIS when:

- The study is diagnostic (imaging of the brain, spine, head and neck, or peripheral nervous system), a neuroimaging technique, or basic science — AJNR's scope covers clinical imaging, therapy, and basic science of the nervous system, while JNIS's scope is neurointerventional treatment.
- The study spans diagnostic and interventional neuroradiology — AJNR's scope includes interventional procedures and techniques in neurointervention alongside imaging.

### Choose JNIS over AJNR when:

- The study is about neurointerventional treatment (stroke, aneurysms, brain tumors, spinal disorders) and its audience is neurointerventionalists.
