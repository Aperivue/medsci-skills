# Journal Profile: Cancer Research and Treatment

## Journal Identity

- **Full name**: Cancer Research and Treatment
- **Abbreviation**: Cancer Res Treat
- **Publisher**: Korean Cancer Association, with the Korean Society of Medical Oncology and the Korean Association for Lung Cancer
- **ISSN**: 1598-2998 (print), 2005-9256 (online)
- **Frequency**: Quarterly (January 15, April 15, July 15, October 15)
- **Impact Factor**: See the journal homepage for the current JCR value
- **Open Access**: Full OA, CC BY-NC 4.0; article processing charge listed as USD 600 per article and USD 300 for case reports, correspondence, or errata (confirm current amount)
- **Acceptance rate**: Not published by the journal
- **Peer review**: Double-blind; initial decision normally within 4 weeks; revision due within 12 weeks

## Manuscript Types and Word Limits

| Type | Body Word Limit | Abstract | References | Figures/Tables |
|------|----------------|----------|------------|----------------|
| Original Article | No explicit limit stated | 250 words or fewer, structured content | Fewer than 30 | Tables + figures 10 or fewer |
| Review Article | Not stated (usually solicited) | Not stated | Fewer than 30 | Tables + figures 10 or fewer |
| Editorial | 5 pages or fewer | Not stated | Not stated | Not stated |
| Special Article | Not stated | Not stated | Not stated | Not stated |
| Case Report | Not stated | Unstructured, 150 words or fewer | 15 or fewer | 5 or fewer including tables |
| Correspondence | 400 words or fewer (3 authors at most) | None stated | 5 or fewer | 1 table or figure at most |

## Abstract Requirements

Original Articles require an abstract of no more than 250 words, written in one paragraph and describing, in order:

```text
Purpose
Materials and Methods
Results
Conclusion
```

Provide 3 to 10 MeSH keywords.

---

## Required Sections (Original Article)

1. **Title page**
   - Concise informative title, author names, departments and institutions
   - Running title (fewer than 50 characters)
   - Complete corresponding-author contact details
2. **Abstract and keywords**
3. **Introduction**
4. **Materials and Methods**
   - Ethical Statement subsection with IRB title and approval number and informed-consent description
   - Trial registration and data-sharing statement for clinical trials
5. **Results**
6. **Discussion**
7. **Author Contributions** (Conceived and designed the analysis / Collected the data / Contributed data or analysis tools / Performed the analysis / Wrote the paper)
8. **Conflicts of Interest**
9. **Funding**
10. **Acknowledgments** (if needed)
11. **References**
12. **Tables and figure legends**

---

## Statistical Reporting

- Identify statistical measures such as SD or SE in tables.
- Use SI units and standard abbreviations, defined at first use.
- Identify drugs and chemicals precisely, with generic name, dose, route, and manufacturer city/country.
- Follow the applicable reporting guideline: CONSORT, STARD, PRISMA, STROBE, or MOOSE.
- Report exact p-values, 95% confidence intervals, effect sizes, and statistical software with version as the default; these are not itemized in the CRT Instructions.

---

## Figures

- **Maximum**: Tables plus figures must not exceed 10.
- **Resolution**: Preferably 300 dpi for figures and 600 dpi for line art and graphs.
- **Format**: EPS or TIF preferred; JPG accepted for color figures.
- **Size**: Column width of 8.5 cm or 17.5 cm.
- **Color**: Permitted.
- **Other**: Upload each figure as a separate file and keep legends in the manuscript. Use Fig. 1A, Fig. 1B for multi-panel figures. Give stain and magnification for light microscopy and an internal scale marker for electron microscopy. Supplementary items are cited as S1 Table, S2 Fig.

## Common Rejection Reasons

_Editorial-risk summary drawn from the official instructions._

1. Limited originality or insufficient importance to experimental and clinical cancer research.
2. Unregistered interventional clinical trial, or incomplete ethics, IRB, consent, or data-sharing information.
3. Missing mandatory declaration of AI-assisted technologies, or prohibited generative-AI image creation or alteration.
4. Inadequate English; manuscripts from non-English-speaking countries must be revised by a professional linguistic reviewer, evidenced in the cover letter.
5. Noncompliance with structure, the 250-word abstract, the under-30-reference cap, or the combined 10 tables-plus-figures limit.

---

## Cover Letter

Should include:
- Article type and a concise statement of relevance to CRT's experimental or clinical oncology scope.
- Originality and exclusive-submission declaration.
- Explicit evidence that professional linguistic review was performed, when submitting from a non-English-speaking country.
- Disclosure of funding, conflicts of interest, and any AI-assisted technologies used.
- Ethics approval, informed consent, trial registration, and data-sharing status where applicable.

---

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy

- **Requirement level:** Required.
- **Permitted scope:** Generative AI may be used to enhance readability and language accuracy; chatbots and AI tools cannot be listed as authors; AI-generated material cannot be cited as a primary source.
- **Disclosure location:** Declared at submission and clearly reported in the manuscript, specifying tool, model name, version, manufacturer, and the capacity in which it was used.
- **AI-generated images:** Not permitted for creating or altering images, except where AI is integral to the research design or methods and is described in Methods. Basic brightness, contrast, or color adjustments are acceptable if original information is preserved; pre-adjustment images may be requested.
- **Policy URL:** https://www.e-crt.org/authors/authors.php

## Positioning

Submit here for solid clinical or translational oncology work across tumor types, including molecular oncology, biomarkers, epidemiology, screening, therapeutics, and real-world clinical outcomes. It is a practical target for well-executed Korean or Asian multicenter oncology research that carries international relevance, and a reasonable one-tier-down option after a desk reject at a highly selective international oncology venue.

Do not prioritize CRT for a narrowly breast-specific manuscript better served by a dedicated breast journal, or for a practice-changing global trial whose novelty supports a more selective international venue.

| Dimension | Cancer Research and Treatment | ESMO Open | Journal of Breast Cancer |
|-----------|-------------------------------|-----------|--------------------------|
| Society | Korean Cancer Association, KSMO, Korean Association for Lung Cancer | ESMO | Korean Breast Cancer Society |
| Scope | Experimental and clinical oncology across cancer types | Broad international clinical oncology | Breast-specific clinical and translational research |
| Impact factor | See journal homepage | 10.6 (JCR 2025) | 2.4 (2024) |
| Emphasis | Broad oncology, translational-to-clinical continuum, open access | International oncology audience, broad clinical impact | Breast specificity, regional breast data |
