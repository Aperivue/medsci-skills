# Review macro skeleton — reference template

The skeleton depends on the format confirmed in Step 0. Narrative reviews use the 7-part
skeleton. Scoping and systematic reviews use IMRaD, with one Methods and one Results slot per
reporting item. A tag `[PRISMA-ScR n]` or `[PRISMA 2020 n]` names the item number in the
checklist that `/check-reporting` audits against:
`skills/check-reporting/references/checklists/PRISMA_ScR.md` and
`skills/check-reporting/references/checklists/PRISMA_2020.md`. Keep a slot even when it will
read "not done" or "not prepared": an empty slot is visible to the author, a missing one is not.

## Format: narrative

7-part skeleton (SANRA is an appraisal aid, not a reporting checklist; no PRISMA slots).

1. Abstract  2. Introduction (scope + non-overlap)  3. Background
4. Thematic body by spine axis (+ summary table per section)
5. Frontiers  6. Challenges/Discussion (+ evaluation-metrics critique)  7. Conclusion

Tables: model-comparison summary table per body section.
Figures: conceptual schematic; landscape/timeline.

## Format: scoping

IMRaD, PRISMA-ScR. Item numbers 13, 15, 16, 22 and 23 are not applicable to scoping reviews and
get no slot.

- Title — identified as a scoping review [PRISMA-ScR 1]
- Abstract — structured summary [PRISMA-ScR 2]
- Introduction
  - Rationale [PRISMA-ScR 3]
  - Objectives (PCC) + scope and non-overlap block [PRISMA-ScR 4]
- Methods
  - Protocol and registration [PRISMA-ScR 5]
  - Eligibility criteria [PRISMA-ScR 6]
  - Information sources [PRISMA-ScR 7]
  - Search [PRISMA-ScR 8]
  - Selection of sources of evidence [PRISMA-ScR 9]
  - Data charting process [PRISMA-ScR 10]
  - Data items [PRISMA-ScR 11]
  - Critical appraisal of individual sources of evidence (optional; state if not done) [PRISMA-ScR 12]
  - Synthesis of results (charting/mapping methods) [PRISMA-ScR 14]
- Results
  - Selection of sources of evidence (flow diagram) [PRISMA-ScR 17]
  - Characteristics of sources of evidence [PRISMA-ScR 18]
  - Critical appraisal within sources of evidence (optional; only if item 12 was done) [PRISMA-ScR 19]
  - Results of individual sources of evidence (charting table) [PRISMA-ScR 20]
  - Synthesis of results — thematic subsections by spine axis, each with a summary table [PRISMA-ScR 21]
- Discussion
  - Summary of evidence [PRISMA-ScR 24]
  - Frontiers; evaluation-metrics critique
  - Limitations [PRISMA-ScR 25]
  - Conclusions (maps the evidence; no recommendation-grade language) [PRISMA-ScR 26]
- Funding [PRISMA-ScR 27]

Tables: charting table; summary table per thematic subsection.
Figures: PRISMA-ScR flow; conceptual schematic; landscape/timeline.

## Format: systematic

IMRaD, PRISMA 2020.

- Title — identified as a systematic review [PRISMA 2020 1]
- Abstract — PRISMA 2020 for Abstracts [PRISMA 2020 2]
- Introduction
  - Rationale [PRISMA 2020 3]
  - Objectives + scope and non-overlap block [PRISMA 2020 4]
- Methods
  - Eligibility criteria [PRISMA 2020 5]
  - Information sources [PRISMA 2020 6]
  - Search strategy [PRISMA 2020 7]
  - Selection process [PRISMA 2020 8]
  - Data collection process [PRISMA 2020 9]
  - Data items — outcomes [PRISMA 2020 10a]; other variables [PRISMA 2020 10b]
  - Study risk of bias assessment [PRISMA 2020 11]
  - Effect measures [PRISMA 2020 12]
  - Synthesis methods [PRISMA 2020 13a] [PRISMA 2020 13b] [PRISMA 2020 13c] [PRISMA 2020 13d] [PRISMA 2020 13e] [PRISMA 2020 13f]
  - Reporting bias assessment [PRISMA 2020 14]
  - Certainty assessment [PRISMA 2020 15]
- Results
  - Study selection (flow diagram) [PRISMA 2020 16a]; excluded near-miss studies [PRISMA 2020 16b]
  - Study characteristics [PRISMA 2020 17]
  - Risk of bias in studies [PRISMA 2020 18]
  - Results of individual studies [PRISMA 2020 19]
  - Results of syntheses — subsections by spine axis [PRISMA 2020 20a] [PRISMA 2020 20b] [PRISMA 2020 20c] [PRISMA 2020 20d]
  - Reporting biases [PRISMA 2020 21]
  - Certainty of evidence [PRISMA 2020 22]
- Discussion — interpretation [PRISMA 2020 23a]; limitations of the evidence [PRISMA 2020 23b];
  limitations of the review process [PRISMA 2020 23c]; implications [PRISMA 2020 23d];
  evaluation-metrics critique
- Other information — registration and protocol [PRISMA 2020 24a] [PRISMA 2020 24b] [PRISMA 2020 24c];
  support [PRISMA 2020 25]; competing interests [PRISMA 2020 26]; availability of data, code and
  other materials [PRISMA 2020 27]

Tables: study-characteristics table; extraction table; risk-of-bias table.
Figures: PRISMA 2020 flow.
