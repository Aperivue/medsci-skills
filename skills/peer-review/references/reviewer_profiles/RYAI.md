# Radiology: Artificial Intelligence (RYAI) — Reviewer Profile

Only what Radiology: Artificial Intelligence publishes, with the page each item comes from.
Recommendation options and scorecard fields are not recorded here (see [README](README.md)).

## Journal

- **Publisher:** Radiological Society of North America (RSNA)
- **Submission system:** ScholarOne — [Instructions for Authors](https://pubs.rsna.org/page/ai/author-instructions)
- **Review model:** double-anonymized — [Instructions for Authors](https://pubs.rsna.org/page/ai/author-instructions).
  Do not guess at, or refer to, the authors' identity.

## Comment structure

Source: [Reviewer Toolkit](https://pubs.rsna.org/page/ai/reviewers).

The peer-review system asks a series of questions to guide the review and requests comments in
two boxes, one for the editor and one for the authors; comments can be typed, pasted, or uploaded
as a Word or PDF file. The toolkit gives no fixed heading order and links RSNA's own articles on
writing a review for *Radiology*. Use the generic two-block format in SKILL.md Phase 3.

## Reviewer AI policy

From the Reviewer Toolkit, "RSNA Journals Confidentiality and AI Policy":

- Reviewers follow the ICMJE recommendations for reviewers.
- Do not upload manuscripts to software or other AI technologies where confidentiality cannot be
  assured.
- Some AI tools may be permissible for writing, grammar and language, but only with the editorial
  office's approval before the review starts.

Sources: [Reviewer Toolkit](https://pubs.rsna.org/page/ai/reviewers) and RSNA's
[Large Language Models policy](https://pubs.rsna.org/page/policies#llm).
