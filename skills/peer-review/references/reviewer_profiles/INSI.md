# Insights into Imaging (INSI) — Reviewer Profile

Only what Insights into Imaging publishes, with the page each item comes from. Recommendation
options and scorecard fields are not recorded here (see [README](README.md)).

## Journal

- **Society:** European Society of Radiology (ESR)
- **Submission system:** Editorial Manager — [For Authors](https://journals.myesr.org/insights-imaging/for-authors/)
- **Review model:** single-blind since January 2023 — manuscripts reach reviewers unblinded and
  reviewer names are not shown to authors.
  [For Reviewers](https://journals.myesr.org/insights-imaging/for-reviewers/). A later line on the
  same page still says double-blind; either way, do not sign the review.

## Comment structure

Source: [For Reviewers](https://journals.myesr.org/insights-imaging/for-reviewers/).

The page names two text parts, **Feedback to Authors** and **Confidential Comments to the
Editor**, both in professional, neutral language. It gives no fixed heading order; its review
checklist asks:

- What are the paper's major messages? Will they interest the medical community?
- Is the paper innovative and of practical value?
- Does its organisation follow its stated objectives?
- Did the authors state relevant professional experience with the cases they evaluated?
- Could any statement lead to misconceptions?
- Does each figure add value, and could it be improved?
- Are the references recent and correctly cited?

Qualities of the report: specific instructions rather than general remarks; constructive
suggestions; minor changes kept apart from major issues; a fair, respectful tone; no publish /
do-not-publish suggestion in the feedback to authors; no language corrections — tell the Editor in
Chief if poor writing could lead to the science being misunderstood.

**Decision guidance** (same page): a methodological question, a potential misunderstanding or a
major discrepancy between parts of the manuscript points to "Reject & Resubmit"; changes for
clarity or readability point to a revision; several minor points can make a minor revision a
major one; "Accept" is usually not appropriate for a first revision.

## Reviewer AI policy

The ESR Journals editors' joint statement on large language models, published simultaneously in
European Radiology, Insights into Imaging and European Radiology Experimental: reviewers should not
upload manuscripts to software or AI-assisted tools where confidentiality cannot be assured, and a
reviewer who uses an AI tool in a way that does not breach confidentiality must name the tool and
say how it was used. — [Insights into Imaging](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13244-023-01600-9)
